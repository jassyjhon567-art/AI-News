Bloom Security Launches from Stealth with $20M to Protect AI-Native Endpoints

www.unite.ai · Thu, 30 Jul 2026 16:59:37 +0000

Bloom Security has emerged from stealth with $20 million in seed funding to protect enterprise AI-native endpoints, including AI agents and local automation tools on employee devices.

Cybersecurity startup Bloom Security has officially emerged from stealth mode today, announcing $20 million in seed funding to address a critical and rapidly expanding blind spot in modern enterprise cybersecurity. As corporate environments rapidly embrace artificial intelligence, employee devices are increasingly populated by an unmanaged ecosystem of AI agents, browser extensions, plugins, code packages, and local automation tools. Bloom Security was founded specifically to secure these emerging AI-native endpoints against novel threats, data leaks, and security vulnerabilities that conventional security frameworks struggle to identify or mitigate effectively.

The oversubscribed funding round was led by Glilot Capital Partners, a prominent venture capital firm, alongside active participation from Ten Eleven Ventures, Okta Ventures, and Runtime Ventures. The investment also drew strong support from high-profile angel investors, including the founders and key leaders of established cybersecurity innovators such as Dig Security, Demisto, Snyk, and Talon Cyber Security. This substantial early-stage financial support underscores the industry's urgent demand for specialized security architectures tailored to handle the complexities of decentralized AI tools deployed across employee endpoints.

As modern organizations push to integrate generative AI tools directly into daily operational workflows, maintaining visibility and control over endpoint activities has become an imperative challenge for enterprise security leaders. Bloom Security plans to utilize the fresh capital to accelerate the development of its core technology platform, expand its specialized engineering and threat research teams, and scale its market go-to-market operations. By delivering targeted visibility and proactive risk management, the company aims to help global enterprises confidently adopt AI technologies without compromising corporate security posture.
