Dyson Expands Hair Care Lineup with Debut Dryer Brush and Next-Generation Straightener

www.cnet.com · 2026-09-03T15:01:06Z

Dyson has unveiled its first dryer brush and a redesigned straightener, offering versatile styling tools engineered to deliver sleek, frizz-free hair without causing heat damage.

Dyson has officially expanded its personal care portfolio by unveiling its first-ever hot dryer brush alongside a versatile, newly redesigned hair straightener. Renowned for applying advanced aerodynamic engineering principles to everyday beauty routines, the technology company continues to reshape the landscape of premium hair styling and grooming appliances. This latest release marks a strategic effort to cater to growing consumer demand for multifunctional styling devices capable of delivering salon-grade results effortlessly within the comfort of home.

The newly introduced dryer brush combines the rapid performance of a blow dryer with the smoothing and shaping capabilities of a professional rounded styling brush. Engineered with intelligent temperature regulation and sophisticated airflow distribution, the tool effortlessly styles hair from damp to dry while actively preventing excessive heat exposure and stubborn frizz. Alongside the brush, the upgraded hair straightener incorporates precision-engineered plates designed to distribute uniform tension and heat across strands, ensuring a sleek, soft finish that effectively preserves natural moisture and structural strength.

This comprehensive double product launch aligns directly with an ongoing industry-wide transition toward wellness-focused hair care technology that replaces extreme heat with controlled air dynamics and gentler mechanics. By providing versatile solutions that substantially cut down on daily styling time while protecting delicate hair fibers from long-term thermal damage and breakage, Dyson successfully addresses the primary priorities of modern consumers. The innovative new lineup demonstrates the company's sustained commitment to beauty science and is scheduled to roll out across international retail markets in the coming months.
