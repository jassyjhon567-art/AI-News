Firebird Launches Global AI Factory Platform with 2-Gigawatt Expansion Pipeline

www.unite.ai · Sat, 08 Aug 2026 21:58:02 +0000

Firebird has launched its first AI factory in Armenia and announced plans for a 2-gigawatt global infrastructure pipeline by 2028, supported by expansion into Kazakhstan and NVIDIA investment.

Firebird has officially launched its global AI infrastructure initiative with the inauguration of its first operational AI factory in Hrazdan, Armenia. During the opening ceremony on August 8, 2026, the company unveiled its strategic roadmap aimed at expanding high-performance computing facilities internationally. The newly established Hrazdan facility serves as the initial operational debut for Firebird's platform, setting the foundation for scalable, high-density artificial intelligence compute infrastructure designed to meet growing global technology demands.

The company plans to scale the flagship Armenia site significantly, aiming to host more than 70,000 NVIDIA Rubin and Blackwell GPUs while expanding its power capacity beyond 300 megawatts. As part of its broader international strategy, Firebird also highlighted its entry into a second key market in Kazakhstan, where it has successfully secured 125 megawatts of capacity. Furthermore, the announcement revealed an intended strategic investment from tech giant NVIDIA, which will help accelerate Firebird's ambitious deployment timeline and hardware integration across its expanding data center network.

Looking toward the future, Firebird aims to construct a massive pipeline reaching 2 gigawatts of total AI infrastructure capacity by the end of 2028. This rapid scaling strategy addresses the critical global shortage of high-density data centers capable of supporting next-generation machine learning models. By strategically deploying facilities across emerging tech hubs with access to substantial power resources, Firebird is positioning itself as a key foundational player in the rapidly evolving artificial intelligence landscape.
