Buy Now, Pay Later Surges to $157 Billion: Why Payments Are Becoming Core to the Product Experience

www.inc.com · Mon, 12 Oct 2026 16:50:56 -0400

Buy Now, Pay Later has evolved into a major market force, requiring businesses to integrate financing directly into their core product experience rather than treating it as a checkout afterthought.

The rapid expansion of Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL) has transformed what was once a niche checkout option into a massive industry valued at roughly $157 billion. Across digital commerce, flexible installment options have altered modern consumer habits, shifting how shoppers evaluate price tags and purchasing power. Rather than assessing affordability only at the final payment gateway, consumers now factor financing options into their initial consideration during the discovery phase.

This structural shift influences the entire customer journey, from basket size and product selection to post-purchase interactions. Financing is no longer separated from product utility; it directly impacts how buyers perceive value, handle returns, and build trust with a brand. When integrated smoothly into the browsing process, BNPL lowers friction and increases conversion, whereas cumbersome payment systems risk alienating potential buyers before they reach the cart.

Consequently, modern business leaders must rethink their approach to payments and digital strategy. Treating financing as a back-end utility or a final transactional checkbox is no longer viable in a competitive market. Instead, companies must view flexible payment solutions as an essential component of their core product offering, creating transparent, accessible, and customer-centric retail experiences.
