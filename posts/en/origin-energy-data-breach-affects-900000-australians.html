Origin Energy Data Breach Impacts Nearly One Million Australians Following Cyberattack

www.securityweek.com · Tue, 28 Jul 2026 05:01:05 +0000

Major Australian provider Origin Energy is investigating a severe data breach affecting up to 900,000 individuals after hackers claimed to have compromised systems and stolen millions of customer records.

Australian energy provider Origin Energy is facing a major cybersecurity incident following a significant data breach that has impacted approximately 900,000 individuals across the country. According to reports, a threat actor managed to breach the company's internal systems and subsequently claimed to have exfiltrated sensitive personal information belonging to as many as two million customers. The incident highlights the continuing cyber threats targeting critical national infrastructure and utility companies.

While the full extent of the compromised data is still being verified, cyber breaches of this nature typically involve sensitive user information such as names, contact details, and financial account identifiers. The breach has raised serious concerns among regulators and consumer advocacy groups regarding the safeguard measures implemented by major service providers. Australia has experienced a surge in high-profile cyberattacks recently, putting increased pressure on corporations to fortify their digital defenses against sophisticated actors.

In response to the security compromise, Origin Energy has initiated a comprehensive investigation to determine the precise vector of attack and the scope of affected records. The company is working alongside cybersecurity experts and relevant regulatory authorities to contain the incident and secure its network infrastructure. Affected customers are being urged to monitor their accounts for suspicious activity, update authentication credentials, and remain cautious of potential phishing attempts attempting to exploit the situation.
