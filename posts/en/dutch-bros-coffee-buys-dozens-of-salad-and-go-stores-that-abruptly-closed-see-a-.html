Dutch Bros Acquires 51 Salad and Go Drive-Thru Locations for $105 Million Following Bankruptcy

www.fastcompany.com · 2026-08-05T21:55:00

Dutch Bros Coffee has agreed to purchase 51 former Salad and Go drive-thru locations for $105 million following the salad chain's sudden bankruptcy and operational closure.

Following the abrupt Chapter 11 bankruptcy filing and complete operational shutdown of drive-thru chain Salad and Go, Dutch Bros Coffee has stepped in to acquire dozens of its former real estate locations. Overleveraged following an aggressive expansion in 2021 and severely impacted by a recent health outbreak, Salad and Go officially closed all remaining stores this week. In response, Arizona-headquartered Dutch Bros agreed to pay $105 million to acquire leases for 51 locations in Arizona and Nevada, alongside additional store leases across Texas and Oklahoma.

The transaction aligns closely with Dutch Bros' ongoing retail strategy, as both chain models feature drive-thru service with no indoor customer seating. Court filings indicate that Salad and Go deliberately sought coffee and beverage operators who could easily utilize the small footprint structures without requiring major kitchen infrastructure such as deep fryers, grills, or exhaust hoods. Dutch Bros, which currently operates roughly 1,225 locations nationwide, won the competitive bidding process against another rival coffee brand to secure the real estate assets.

Pending final bankruptcy court approval, Dutch Bros plans to begin converting the acquired drive-thru units into coffee outlets starting next year. The announcement coincided with the company's second-quarter financial earnings report, after which its stock dipped over 13 percent in after-hours trading. The majority of the acquired properties are situated in major metropolitan markets, including Phoenix and Las Vegas, allowing Dutch Bros to rapidly scale its footprint across key Southwestern regional markets.
