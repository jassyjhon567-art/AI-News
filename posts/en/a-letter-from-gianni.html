Max Flannel’s signature blend of pragmatic advice and lighthearted humor provides readers with a fresh perspective on managing difficult colleagues, negotiating workplace boundaries, and advancing their careers effectively.

Readers across various industries have warmly welcomed the announcement, eager to once again submit their personal workplace queries. Gianni’s letter underscores the publication’s commitment to addressing the human side of business and work culture.
