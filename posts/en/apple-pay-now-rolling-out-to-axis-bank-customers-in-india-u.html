Apple Pay Officially Launches in India, Beginning with Axis Bank Customers

9to5mac.com · Wed, 30 Sep 2026 01:19:30 +0000

Apple Pay has launched in India for Axis Bank Visa and Mastercard credit cardholders, bringing secure contactless payments to iPhone and Apple Watch users across the country.

Apple has officially begun the rollout of its widely anticipated mobile payment service, Apple Pay, in India. The initial launch phase is currently available to eligible cardholders of Axis Bank, marking a major milestone in Apple’s ongoing service expansion across one of the world's most dynamic and fastest-growing digital payment landscapes.

The tech giant confirmed that customers holding Axis Bank-issued Visa and Mastercard credit cards can now add their cards directly to the Apple Wallet app. Once provisioned, users can conduct seamless, contactless transactions at physical retail point-of-sale terminals, within supported iOS applications, and across participating online merchant platforms using their iPhone or Apple Watch. These payments utilize Apple's tokenization architecture alongside biometric authentication via Face ID or Touch ID, offering strong privacy and fraud prevention.

This launch arrives nearly twelve years after Apple first introduced Apple Pay to the global market, addressing a long-standing gap in the company’s regional service portfolio. Although India’s digital transaction ecosystem is primarily driven by the Unified Payments Interface (UPI), the arrival of Apple Pay delivers a streamlined, secure experience for traditional credit card transactions. Industry observers anticipate that Apple will expand support to more domestic banks and card networks in the near future.
