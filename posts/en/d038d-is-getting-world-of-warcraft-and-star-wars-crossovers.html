Dungeons & Dragons Announces Major Multiverse Crossovers with World of Warcraft and Star Wars

www.theverge.com · 2026-07-31T08:31:00-04:00

Wizards of the Coast has announced major Dungeons & Dragons crossover sourcebooks featuring World of Warcraft and Star Wars, expanding the iconic tabletop game into massive new entertainment universes.

Wizards of the Coast has officially unveiled its most ambitious multiverse initiative to date for Dungeons & Dragons, expanding the iconic tabletop roleplaying game into new pop culture realms. The publisher announced a series of upcoming crossover sourcebooks that will seamlessly integrate the vast lore of Blizzard Entertainment's World of Warcraft alongside the expansive universe of Star Wars into the D&D system. This strategic move marks a major milestone in combining classic tabletop gaming with world-renowned digital and cinematic franchises.

The initiative begins with a comprehensive World of Warcraft sourcebook, bringing the legendary world of Azeroth directly to player gaming tables. Tabletop enthusiasts will soon be able to explore iconic locations, adopt classic character classes, and battle legendary creatures from the Warcraft mythos using refined fifth-edition mechanics. Following this fantasy crossover, the upcoming Star Wars expansion will introduce futuristic sci-fi elements, specialized Force mechanics, and galactic adventures tailored specifically for tabletop roleplaying fans.

While franchise crossovers are not entirely new to Dungeons & Dragons, which has previously incorporated settings from Magic: The Gathering, this current initiative represents an unprecedented scale of collaboration with major global entertainment properties. By bringing these massive universes into a unified gaming framework, Wizards of the Coast aims to bridge the gap between different fandoms. The new releases are expected to attract veteran players while inviting an entirely new audience to experience tabletop adventures.
