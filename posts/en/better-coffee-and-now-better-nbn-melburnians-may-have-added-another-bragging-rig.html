Melbourne Outpaces Sydney in Fixed-Line Internet Speeds, New Data Shows

www.techradar.com · Mon, 10 Aug 2026 23:49:23 +0000

Recent data from Ookla reveals Melbourne currently leads Sydney in median fixed-line internet speeds, though experts note infrastructure differences significantly impact individual user experiences across both cities.

Melbourne has claimed a new point of pride over its rival city Sydney, with recent telecommunications data revealing that Australia's southern cultural capital currently enjoys faster median fixed-line internet speeds. According to newly published metrics from global speed testing authority Ookla, Melbourne residents are experiencing slightly higher download and upload performance on average. This revelation adds a technological dimension to the long-standing friendly rivalry between Australia's two largest metropolitan hubs, which historically focused primarily on lifestyle, culture, and coffee quality.

However, industry experts caution that median speed test results offer only a snapshot of general network performance rather than the complete story. Internet connectivity across both major cities is influenced by a complex array of factors, including the local density of infrastructure, the specific mix of broadband technologies deployed, and fluctuating user demand during peak evening hours. Neighbourhoods equipped with direct fiber-to-the-premises upgrades naturally report superior speeds compared to areas still reliant on older copper-based National Broadband Network (NBN) architecture, creating localized speed discrepancies in both cities.

Despite these underlying technical nuances, the latest figures highlight the broader progress being made as Australia continually upgrades its digital infrastructure. With flexible working arrangements and high-bandwidth streaming becoming permanent fixtures of modern daily life, equitable access to high-speed internet remains a crucial priority for urban development. While Melburnians can temporarily boast about superior online performance, ongoing NBN network enhancements across Sydney ensure that the competition for digital dominance between these two economic powerhouses remains dynamic and far from settled.
