WhistlePig's Aged Rye Wins Whiskey of the Year 15 Years After Bold Gamble

www.inc.com · Wed, 19 Aug 2026 13:36:05 -0400

Vermont craft distillery WhistlePig earned top honors at the USA Spirits Ratings, with its flagship aged rye being crowned Whiskey of the Year.

Fifteen years ago, Vermont-based distillery WhistlePig took a major gamble by focusing heavily on aged rye whiskey at a time when the category was largely overlooked. That long-term commitment to quality and patience has culminated in one of the industry's highest honors, as the brand's flagship bottle was recently crowned Whiskey of the Year at the prestigious USA Spirits Ratings.

In addition to the top overall award, the craft distiller secured three major honors at this year's competition. The panel of expert judges evaluated submissions based on three core criteria: quality, value for money, and packaging. WhistlePig's flagship aged rye received exceptional marks across all categories, earning special praise from evaluators for its rich, complex flavor profile and its standout, sophisticated bottle design.

This triumph underscores the evolution of the American craft distilling landscape and cements WhistlePig's position at the forefront of the rye renaissance. What started as an ambitious, non-traditional venture a decade and a half ago has now set a gold standard in the spirits industry, proving that premium aged rye holds broad and enduring appeal among connoisseurs and casual consumers alike.
