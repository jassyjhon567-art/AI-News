Why Relying on Passwords Is a Major Security Risk and How Passkeys Offer a Safer Alternative

www.pcmag.com · Fri, 09 Oct 26 13:19:52 +0000

Adopting cryptographic passkeys and practicing active session cookie management provides essential protection against modern cyber threats, rendering traditional, vulnerable passwords obsolete.

For decades, traditional passwords have served as the primary line of defense for digital accounts. However, cybersecurity experts increasingly warn that standard alphanumeric credentials are now a major liability. With sophisticated phishing schemes, widespread credential stuffing, and automated brute-force attacks becoming commonplace, conventional passwords offer minimal protection against determined cybercriminals. When users reuse passwords across multiple platforms, a single compromised service can jeopardize their entire online identity.

To counter these persistent vulnerabilities, the tech industry is rapidly shifting toward passkeys. Built on robust public-key cryptography standards, passkeys replace memorized words and numbers with a unique pair of cryptographic keys. The private key remains securely stored on the user's personal device, accessible only through biometric authentication or a local device PIN, while the public key resides on the website's server. Because the private key is never transmitted over the internet or saved on an external system, attackers cannot harvest or steal it through traditional phishing or server-side data breaches.

In addition to adopting passkeys, internet users must also remain vigilant about session management and browser data. Attackers frequently bypass login defenses by stealing session cookies, which grant unauthorized access without requiring credentials. Regularly clearing stored cookies, configuring browsers to terminate idle sessions, and utilizing privacy-focused security tools are practical steps to neutralize this threat. By transitioning to cryptographic passkeys and actively managing digital sessions, users can effectively shut out unauthorized intruders and secure their valuable information.
