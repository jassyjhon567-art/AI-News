How AI Can Serve as a Thought Partner to Supercharge Creative Problem-Solving

www.fastcompany.com · 2026-08-03T05:00:00

Using Large Language Models to abstract complex problems and discover cross-disciplinary analogies helps professionals unlock creative solutions while simultaneously training their own critical thinking skills.

Professional routine often relies on past experience, as familiarity provides ready-made solutions for everyday challenges. However, when unfamiliar situations arise that fall outside past occurrences, true creative problem-solving becomes essential. A key pathway to creative thinking involves drawing powerful analogies from unrelated fields to gain fresh insights. The primary obstacle is that individuals frequently get trapped in the specific, granular details of their immediate problems. Utilizing Large Language Models (LLMs) helps users strip away these specific details, guiding them to distill a complex issue down to its basic, abstract essence so they can reframe their thinking effectively.

After establishing a clear, abstract definition of a problem, an LLM can serve as an interactive thinking partner to discover and analyze compelling analogies. For example, viewing a weed-killer development challenge as a problem of "selective destruction" allows one to explore analogies in oncology, where medical treatments must destroy target cells without harming healthy surrounding tissue. Through iterative prompts, users can brainstorm with the AI, test potential analogies, explore relevant cross-disciplinary literature, and examine novel conceptual perspectives that would not otherwise be obvious, ultimately unlocking innovative avenues for problem resolution.

Leveraging artificial intelligence in this interactive manner offers benefits far beyond simply reaching a swift solution. Rather than treating AI as a shortcut to generate final answers, using it as a cognitive scaffold actively enhances a professional's personal creative skills over time. Practicing abstract thinking alongside a language model sharpens an individual's independent problem-solving capabilities, enabling better pattern recognition even without digital assistance. Furthermore, as human input becomes more sophisticated, the AI provides increasingly nuanced feedback, establishing a mutually reinforcing cycle that continuously improves creative output.
