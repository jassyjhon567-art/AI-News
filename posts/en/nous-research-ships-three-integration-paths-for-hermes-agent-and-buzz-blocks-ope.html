Nous Research Unveils Three Integration Paths Connecting Hermes Agent with Block’s Buzz Workspace

www.marktechpost.com · Fri, 31 Jul 2026 09:08:13 +0000

Nous Research has integrated its Hermes Agent into Block's open-source Nostr workspace Buzz through three distinct deployment paths, enhancing human-AI collaboration across decentralized communication channels.

Leading artificial intelligence research organization Nous Research has officially introduced native support for its Hermes Agent within Buzz, an open-source and self-hostable Nostr workspace created by Block. Buzz is explicitly engineered as a dynamic, shared environment where human team members and autonomous AI entities can interact directly, exchange information, and execute tasks within unified communication channels. This strategic integration is designed to bridge decentralized messaging infrastructure with advanced autonomous system capabilities, establishing a secure and cohesive digital environment for modern workflow automation.

To facilitate smooth and flexible deployment across varied technical environments, Nous Research has delivered three distinct integration pathways tailored to specific operational requirements. The available deployment models comprise a Desktop runtime environment designed for localized testing and execution, a specialized relay bridge tailored for decentralized message routing, and a comprehensive native gateway platform. These versatile options empower software developers, system architects, and organizations to seamlessly incorporate sophisticated AI agents into their messaging frameworks based on their unique technical infrastructure, security models, and operational preferences.

Crucially, the native gateway architecture ensures that the core operational strengths of the Hermes Agent remain fully intact throughout the Buzz workspace. Vital functionality—including persistent long-term memory retention, custom skills execution, human approval workflows for sensitive actions, and scheduled cron task delivery—continues to function without interruption inside the shared channels. By successfully connecting open-source Nostr communication protocols with highly adaptable artificial intelligence toolsets, this release represents a meaningful step forward for transparent and decentralized human-AI collaboration.
