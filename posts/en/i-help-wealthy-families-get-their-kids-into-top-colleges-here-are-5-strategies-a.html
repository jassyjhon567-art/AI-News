Top College Admissions Consultant Shares Key Strategies for Every Parent

www.businessinsider.com · 2026-07-27T09:31:01Z

Elite admissions consultant Lindsay Tanne Howe outlines accessible strategies for parents to help their children secure spots at top universities by fostering intellectual curiosity and authentic engagement.

Lindsay Tanne Howe is an elite college admissions consultant who has spent years advising ultra-wealthy families on how to secure coveted spots at top-tier universities. While high-net-worth families frequently have access to specialized resources, exclusive networks, and highly personalized guidance, Howe emphasizes that the foundational principles for academic success are entirely universal. As global competition for prestigious higher education intensifies, she insists that effective strategies do not rely solely on wealth, but rather on deliberate, thoughtful parenting techniques that any family can implement.

Among her primary recommendations is the early cultivation of genuine intellectual curiosity and a passionate commitment to learning. Rather than encouraging students to artificially pad their college applications with dozens of superficial extracurricular activities, Howe strongly advises focusing on deep, meaningful engagement in a few specific areas of interest. Developing consistent reading habits, supporting independent research or creative projects, and nurturing an authentic desire to explore complex subjects can differentiate applicants far more effectively than merely accumulating impressive-sounding titles or certificates.

Additionally, successfully navigating today's complex, holistic admissions process requires long-term strategic planning, honest personal storytelling, and unwavering academic fundamentals. Howe stresses that parents should concentrate on building their children's intrinsic motivation, analytical thinking abilities, and emotional resilience. By implementing these practical, high-impact strategies early in a student's educational journey, parents from all socioeconomic backgrounds can position their children to thrive in the competitive higher education landscape and achieve long-term academic excellence.
