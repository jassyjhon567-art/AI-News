Apple Increases Trade-In Values for iPhones, iPads, Macs, and Apple Watches

9to5mac.com · Thu, 06 Aug 2026 13:35:15 +0000

Apple has updated its official trade-in program, offering higher valuations for select iPhones, iPads, Macs, Apple Watches, and Android devices to encourage upgrades and recycling.

Apple has officially updated its device trade-in program, offering increased estimated values for customers trading in a wide range of hardware, including select iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch models. This strategic adjustment allows consumers to receive higher promotional credits toward the purchase of brand-new Apple devices when returning their older, eligible equipment. In addition to its own hardware lineup, the tech giant has also adjusted trade-in valuations for select Android smartphones, making it noticeably more financially enticing for users from competing mobile platforms to make the switch to Apple's ecosystem.

The revised trade-in schedule spans multiple product generations, offering varying degrees of value increases across different device categories. High-end hardware offerings, such as recent pro-level iPhone models, modern iPad Pro revisions, and powerful Mac laptops, reflect some of the most significant credit boosts in this latest update. As is standard practice, the final trade-in value awarded remains strictly subject to the physical condition, operational functionality, and storage capacity of the returned device, with fully operational and well-maintained items securing the maximum possible trade-in allowance from the company.

Customers looking to upgrade can access these updated valuations directly through Apple's official online storefront or by visiting participating retail store locations worldwide. The calculated trade-in credit can be applied immediately as an instant price reduction against a new device purchase or issued as an Apple Gift Card for future transactions. By periodically updating these value estimates, Apple aims to encourage consumer trade-ins, drive hardware upgrades, and support its long-term environmental sustainability goals by keeping electronic waste out of landfills.
