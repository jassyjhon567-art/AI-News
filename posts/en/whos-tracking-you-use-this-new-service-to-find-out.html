Who Is Tracking You Online? A New Free Tool Reveals the Adtech Entities Behind Web and App Ads

krebsonsecurity.com · Fri, 14 Aug 2026 11:24:35 +0000

A new free platform named DecryptAds analyzes adtech metadata to help users easily discover which third-party entities and advertising networks are tracking their online activities.

Determining who is responsible for serving targeted advertisements across websites or silently harvesting personal information from mobile applications has long been an opaque and daunting challenge for ordinary internet users. Although the underlying digital advertising ecosystem operates using semi-public registries, seller declarations, and supply chain records, this data is heavily fragmented across complex networks. Consequently, understanding exactly which third-party companies monitor your browsing habits and digital behavior has remained nearly impossible for the average consumer.

Historically, the vast majority of digital advertising supply chain information has remained effectively walled away inside systems controlled by dominant tech platforms and specialized adtech brokers. As online surveillance grows increasingly pervasive across both web and mobile environments, mapping how personal identifiers move from publishers to third-party entities is vital for user privacy. Yet, untangling the technical connections among thousands of programmatic advertising vendors has traditionally demanded specialized forensic tools and deep industry expertise that few possess.

A newly launched, free online service named DecryptAds aims to dismantle this transparency barrier by systematically scraping, aggregating, and correlating adtech metadata. The platform simplifies the entire investigative process, enabling everyday internet users, academic researchers, and privacy advocates to quickly uncover the corporate entities tracking their digital footprints. By transforming convoluted advertising supply chains into accessible and searchable records, the tool provides unprecedented clarity on modern digital tracking mechanisms.
