Government Cracks Down on Dark Patterns: Zepto and IndiGo Penalised for Deceptive Practices

www.analyticsinsight.net · Thu, 06 Aug 2026 08:07:05 +0000

Regulatory authorities have penalised major platforms, including Zepto and IndiGo, as part of an intensified crackdown on manipulative dark patterns in digital user interfaces.

The Central Consumer Protection Authority and government regulators have intensified their crackdown on deceptive online practices, penalising major digital platforms including quick-commerce service Zepto and airline operator IndiGo for using dark patterns. These manipulative user interface designs are crafted to trick consumers into making unintentional purchases, paying hidden convenience fees, or surrendering personal data without explicit consent. The action reflects growing regulatory scrutiny over how digital businesses structure their sales funnels to exploit user behavior.

Regulatory bodies identified several non-compliant features across these platforms, such as forced add-ons, misleading countdown timers, pre-ticked checkboxes for optional insurance, and convoluted cancellation processes. Authorities emphasised that such practices violate established consumer rights guidelines issued to foster transparency and fairness in the digital ecosystem. Strict directives and monetary penalties were imposed to ensure immediate compliance, prompting companies to overhaul their digital interfaces and eliminate deceptive design elements.

This enforcement action signals a broader push by consumer protection agencies to safeguard online shoppers and travellers in an increasingly digital economy. Industry experts suggest that the stringent actions against high-profile corporations like Zepto and IndiGo will compel other e-commerce and service platforms to proactively audit their digital user journeys. The government reaffirmed its commitment to maintaining fair trade practices and protecting consumer autonomy across all digital marketplaces.
