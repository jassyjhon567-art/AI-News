GameStop Faces Backlash Over Massive $500 Markups on PS5 Pro Amid Supply Shortages

www.engadget.com · Sat, 10 Oct 2026 16:00:00 +0000

GameStop has drawn heavy criticism from consumers after listing PlayStation 5 Pro consoles for $500 over the standard retail price amid severe hardware shortages.

Video game retailer GameStop has come under fire for listing the newly launched PlayStation 5 Pro at prices substantially higher than the manufacturer’s suggested retail price (MSRP). Facing high consumer interest and limited initial inventory, the retailer appears to be testing buyer willingness by charging markups as high as $500 over the standard retail cost. This pricing approach has drawn widespread attention across the gaming community, where many expected traditional retail channels to maintain standard pricing.

Persistent hardware shortages and production constraints have once again fueled an aggressive resale environment. While consumers have grown accustomed to third-party scalpers charging exorbitant premiums on auction sites and secondary marketplaces, seeing an established, official vendor engage in similar pricing tactics has generated significant backlash. Gamers and industry observers have expressed frustration, pointing out that such markups make an already expensive premium console virtually inaccessible to everyday players.

The decision reflects broader shifts in how legacy retailers are attempting to capture revenue in an era dominated by digital distribution and volatile hardware supplies. By pricing hardware close to prevailing black-market rates, retailers risk alienating their core customer base in exchange for short-term profits. As supply chains gradually stabilize and production ramps up, it remains uncertain whether consumers will capitulate to these inflated prices or simply wait for official inventory to normalize.
