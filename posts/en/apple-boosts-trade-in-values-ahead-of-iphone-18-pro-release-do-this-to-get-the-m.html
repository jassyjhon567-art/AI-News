Apple Increases Trade-In Values Ahead of Anticipated iPhone 18 Pro Launch

www.pcmag.com · Fri, 07 Aug 26 17:21:23 +0000

Apple has boosted device trade-in values ahead of the anticipated iPhone 18 Pro release, prompting users to properly secure their data and compare trade-in offers.

As global anticipation continues to build ahead of the rumored iPhone 18 Pro launch later this year, Apple has strategically boosted its trade-in values to encourage existing smartphone owners to upgrade. The tech giant’s popular trade-in program offers customers a convenient way to hand over their current devices in exchange for instant credit toward a new purchase or an Apple Gift Card. By offering higher payout valuations across a wide range of older iPhone models, Apple aims to strengthen customer retention and incentivize users to remain firmly within its expanding hardware ecosystem before its next flagship smartphone hits the market.

To maximize the financial return and protect personal privacy before shipping off an old device, users must complete several crucial preparation steps. Securing a complete backup of all photos, contacts, and personal data to iCloud or a local computer is essential for a seamless setup process on a new device. Following the backup, users should sign out of their Apple ID account, disable the "Find My" feature, and initiate a full factory reset. This critical step permanently wipes all personal information, safeguarding sensitive data from prospective buyers or recycling personnel.

Although trading in directly with Apple offers unmatched convenience and reliable transactions, consumers are advised to explore alternative selling channels to ensure they obtain the highest possible value. Major wireless carriers, third-party retailers, and dedicated online buyback services frequently launch aggressive promotional campaigns and competitive trade-in bonuses around major product launches. By thoroughly comparing valuation offers from various authorized platforms, buyers can effectively leverage market competition to secure maximum funds toward their upcoming iPhone 18 Pro purchase.
