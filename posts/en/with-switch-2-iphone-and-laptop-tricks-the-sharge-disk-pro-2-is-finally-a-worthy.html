Sharge Disk Pro 2 Ultra Review: A Feature-Packed Everyday Carry SSD for Switch 2, iPhone, and Laptops

www.theverge.com · 2026-07-31T18:09:11-04:00

Sharge's new Disk Pro 2 Ultra combines high-speed portable storage with a 4K HDMI dock, making it a highly versatile everyday carry tool for laptops, iPhones, and gaming consoles.

Sharge, the technology brand widely recognized for its retro Mac-inspired chargers and distinctive transparent battery packs, has officially elevated its portable storage lineup. While earlier iterations—such as the original Sharge Disk, Disk Plus, and first-generation Disk Pro—struggled to fully convince reviewers and everyday users, the new Disk Pro 2 Ultra marks a major turning point for the manufacturer. This latest release shifts the narrative from a simple gimmick to a truly practical everyday carry accessory for modern tech users.

What distinguishes the Disk Pro 2 Ultra from conventional portable solid-state drives is its clever integration of hybrid docking features. Rather than serving solely as a storage device, it doubles as a feature-rich hub featuring a 4K HDMI display port. This dual functionality allows users to connect their laptops, recent iPhone models, and portable handheld devices directly to external monitors or televisions, completely eliminating the need to carry additional USB-C adapters or bulky dongles. Additionally, the unit supports passthrough charging and fast data transfer rates, making it an ideal companion for workflow productivity on the go.

With its robust feature set, the accessory bridges the gap between high-speed data transfer and versatile port expansion. It provides a convenient, single-device solution for mobile professionals, digital creators, and gamers who need external storage for their laptops, smartphones, or next-generation handheld consoles like the rumored Switch 2. As multi-device workflows become the standard, multi-functional tools like the Disk Pro 2 Ultra demonstrate how portable storage can evolve to meet broader consumer demands.
