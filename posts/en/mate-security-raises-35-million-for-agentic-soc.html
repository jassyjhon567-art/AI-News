Mate Security Secures $35 Million Funding to Advance Agentic SOC Technology

www.securityweek.com · Wed, 29 Jul 2026 12:47:13 +0000

Cybersecurity startup Mate Security has raised $35 million to expand its R&D, sales, and customer support teams while advancing its autonomous agentic SOC technology.

Cybersecurity startup Mate Security has announced the successful closure of a $35 million funding round aimed at accelerating the development of its innovative Security Operations Center (SOC) platform. As modern enterprises encounter an ever-evolving landscape of complex cyber threats, the demand for sophisticated, autonomous security operations has reached an all-time high. Mate Security specializes in pioneering "agentic" SOC capabilities, which utilize autonomous artificial intelligence agents to streamline threat detection, simplify security investigations, and execute rapid incident response procedures with minimal human intervention.

According to company representatives, the fresh capital will be strategically allocated to expand internal teams across several key enterprise divisions. A major portion of the investment will be directed toward strengthening the startup's research and development (R&D) arm, enabling continuous product innovation and the deployment of advanced automated features. Simultaneously, Mate Security plans to aggressively scale its sales and customer support operations to accommodate growing market demand, expand its global enterprise customer base, and ensure top-tier service delivery.

This latest influx of funding highlights a growing cybersecurity industry trend toward adopting autonomous AI solutions to alleviate the chronic operational pressure faced by traditional security teams. By integrating agentic systems into daily operations, organizations can dramatically reduce threat detection response times, eliminate severe operational bottlenecks, and empower security analysts to focus on higher-level strategic defense initiatives. With this significant financial backing, Mate Security is well-positioned to cement its role as a key player in shaping the future of automated cybersecurity.
