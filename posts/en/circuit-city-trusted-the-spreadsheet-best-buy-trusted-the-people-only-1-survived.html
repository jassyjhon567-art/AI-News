Circuit City Trusted Spreadsheets While Best Buy Trusted People: A Cautionary AI Era Lesson

www.inc.com · Tue, 28 Jul 2026 17:31:27 -0400

Former Best Buy CEO Hubert Joly warns that companies replacing human workers with AI risk repeating Circuit City's fatal mistake of prioritizing spreadsheet metrics over human value.

In the early 2000s, electronics retail giants Circuit City and Best Buy faced pivotal strategic decisions in an increasingly competitive marketplace. Circuit City chose to prioritize aggressive cost-cutting driven purely by financial spreadsheets, laying off thousands of its most experienced, higher-paid sales associates to boost short-term profit margins. Conversely, Best Buy focused on empowering its workforce, believing that knowledgeable, well-trained, and engaged employees were essential to building lasting customer trust. Ultimately, Circuit City collapsed into liquidation, while Best Buy survived and successfully adapted.

Today, corporate leadership faces a strikingly similar inflection point as companies frantically race to replace human workers with artificial intelligence tools. Former Best Buy Chief Executive Officer Hubert Joly warns that over-relying on automated efficiency risks repeating Circuit City’s fatal mistake. While AI algorithms can optimize routine operations and reduce immediate operational overhead, relying solely on spreadsheet metrics overlooks the irreplaceable value of human empathy, creative problem-solving, and authentic customer relationships that drive long-term brand loyalty.

Ultimately, the contrast between these two retail giants serves as a crucial cautionary lesson for the modern digital age. Advanced technology should be leveraged to augment and enhance human capabilities rather than entirely substitute them. Organizations that view their employees merely as disposable line items on a financial ledger risk diminishing their core value proposition. To achieve sustainable success in an AI-driven economy, business leaders must remember that long-term enterprise value depends on trusting and investing in people.
