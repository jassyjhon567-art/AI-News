Fintech Broker Clear Street Offers Investors Pre-IPO Access to $188 Billion AI Giant Databricks

www.cnbc.com · Fri, 31 Jul 2026 21:49:40 GMT

Fintech brokerage Clear Street has launched a private markets platform offering investors pre-IPO access to late-stage startups, beginning with a stake in $188 billion AI firm Databricks.

Financial technology brokerage firm Clear Street has officially announced the launch of its new private markets platform, aimed at providing institutional and accredited investors with streamlined access to late-stage, high-growth startups. To mark the inauguration of this platform, the broker is facilitating investment access to private equity stakes in Databricks, a prominent artificial intelligence and data management enterprise currently valued at an impressive $188 billion. This strategic move highlights the rising demand among market participants looking to secure positions in leading technology firms before their public market debuts.

Historically, investment opportunities in late-stage private companies have been limited primarily to elite venture capital firms and select institutional funds, leaving a broader range of qualified investors excluded from substantial growth phases. Clear Street's newly established infrastructure seeks to bridge this market gap by simplifying and standardizing the complex secondary trading process for private shares. By facilitating enhanced liquidity and operational efficiency, the brokerage enables eligible investors to diversify their portfolios with high-value technology assets well ahead of traditional initial public offerings.

The choice of Databricks as the platform's premier asset reflects the surging market appetite for scalable enterprise artificial intelligence and data infrastructure solutions. As high-value technology startups increasingly choose to remain private for extended periods to scale their operations, specialized financial platforms like Clear Street are becoming essential components of the modern financial ecosystem. Moving forward, the company intends to broaden its private market offerings, delivering greater transparency and secondary trading opportunities across a wider selection of pre-IPO tech companies.
