Marking a milestone anniversary, ABC is presenting a special live broadcast titled "9/11 Tribute in Light: Live from Lower Manhattan." This special program offers audiences across the United States and around the world an opportunity to pause, reflect, and honor the thousands of innocent lives lost and the brave first responders who sacrificed everything on that fateful day.

Viewers seeking to follow the event live have multiple options across television and digital streaming platforms. The broadcast is accessible through ABC on traditional cable or via local over-the-air antennas. Regardless of how one watches, the Tribute in Light remains an enduring emblem of resilience, unity, and hope in the face of tragedy. As the twin beams of blue light reach miles into the night sky over New York City, the digital stream ensures that global audiences can join together in solemn remembrance.
