Soundcore Sleep A30 Earbuds Receive a $60 Discount, Offering ANC and Extended Battery Life

lifehacker.com · Thu, 15 Oct 2026 17:00:00 +0000

The Soundcore Sleep A30 earbuds are currently available at a $60 discount, featuring active noise cancellation and up to 10 hours of continuous battery life.

The Soundcore Sleep A30 earbuds, designed specifically for nighttime comfort and rest, are currently available with a significant $60 discount. As personal audio technology continues to evolve, dedicated sleep earbuds have emerged as a popular solution for light sleepers, frequent travelers, and anyone struggling with disruptive ambient noise. This promotional reduction makes the specialized hardware substantially more accessible to consumers seeking better sleep hygiene.

The latest iteration introduces active noise cancellation (ANC), addressing one of the most critical demands for overnight audio gear. Alongside passive noise isolation, the addition of ANC helps neutralize unpredictable environmental sounds such as snoring partners, street traffic, or loud neighbors. Furthermore, the earbuds boast up to 10 hours of battery life on a single charge, ensuring that users can enjoy uninterrupted sound masking or soothing audio throughout an entire night without needing to recharge before morning.

Unlike standard wireless earbuds that often cause ear fatigue or pressure when resting on a pillow, the Sleep A30 model emphasizes an ultra-compact, low-profile form factor tailored for side sleepers. With companion software providing customizable sleep sounds and sleep tracking capabilities, the device bridges the gap between traditional earplugs and smart audio wearables. At its current discounted price point, the Soundcore Sleep A30 presents a compelling opportunity for individuals aiming to upgrade their sleep environment.
