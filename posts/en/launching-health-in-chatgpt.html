OpenAI Introduces Health Features in ChatGPT to Offer Personalized Medical Insights

openai.com · Thu, 23 Jul 2026 00:00:00 GMT

Eligible U.S. users can now securely connect their medical records and Apple Health to ChatGPT to receive personalized wellness insights and better navigate their health management.

OpenAI has officially unveiled new health-focused features within ChatGPT, enabling eligible users across the United States to securely connect their electronic medical records and Apple Health data directly to the conversational platform. This milestone integration is designed to help individuals gain deeper, more personalized insights into their physical well-being by transforming raw medical metrics and personal health history into clear, actionable summaries. Through this system, users can engage in interactive conversations about their daily activity, heart rate, sleep patterns, and medical conditions within a single, streamlined interface.

The implementation allows users to seamlessly consolidate disparate health documents, routine lab results, and fitness logs into a unified context for AI-driven analysis. By integrating with trusted frameworks and Apple Health, ChatGPT can assist users in interpreting complex clinical terminology and preparing targeted questions for their upcoming medical appointments. Robust privacy safeguards and strict data protection measures have been built into the system to ensure that sensitive health information remains secure, confidential, and fully under the user's control at all times.

Despite the advanced capabilities of this tool, developers and medical experts emphasize that AI-generated health insights are strictly educational and designed to supplement, rather than replace, professional clinical care and physician advice. The feature is initially rolling out to eligible U.S. consumers, representing a growing shift toward patient-empowered digital healthcare management. As artificial intelligence becomes increasingly integrated into personal wellness, tools like Health in ChatGPT aim to make complex health navigation more accessible and proactive for everyday users.
