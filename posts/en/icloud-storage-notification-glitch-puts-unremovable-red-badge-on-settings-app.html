Persistent iCloud Storage Bug Causes Unremovable Notification Badge on iPhone Settings

9to5mac.com · Tue, 22 Sep 2026 07:40:50 +0000

A persistent notification glitch on iPhones leaves an unremovable red badge on Settings urging users to activate iCloud storage, even if they already possess ample cloud space.

An irritating software glitch is currently affecting a number of iPhone users, causing an unremovable red notification badge to appear over the Settings application icon. The badge is tied to a persistent system prompt asking device owners to "Activate iCloud Storage." However, when users tap on this notification to address it, the system merely directs them to the standard iCloud storage purchase interface, leaving no functional option to dismiss or clear the alert from their screen.

What makes the situation especially frustrating for affected individuals is that the warning seems completely detached from their actual cloud account status. Multiple users who already subscribe to expansive storage tiers—such as premium 2 TB Apple One family bundles with plenty of unused capacity—continue to receive the notification. Even after repeatedly viewing the prompt, toggling account settings, or rebooting their handsets, the stubborn red badge refuses to vanish from the home screen, remaining stuck in an endless loop.

While minor notification bugs occasionally surface across various mobile operating system updates, they remain a distinct nuisance for iPhone owners who prefer an organized, clutter-free digital environment. Inquiries and complaints have begun spreading across online support forums as more people notice the unshakeable alert. Apple has not yet published an official explanation or rolled out an emergency fix targeting this behavior. Until a future software update resolves the underlying cloud sync anomaly, impacted users have little choice but to ignore the persistent and erroneous storage reminder.
