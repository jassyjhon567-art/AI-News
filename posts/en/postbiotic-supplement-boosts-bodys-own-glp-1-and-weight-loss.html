Postbiotic Supplement Shows Promise in Boosting Natural GLP-1 and Aiding Weight Loss

www.newscientist.com · Fri, 07 Aug 2026 13:05:45 +0000

A small trial indicates a postbiotic supplement containing heat-treated bacteria could offer a lower-cost alternative to prescription weight-loss drugs by boosting natural GLP-1 production.

A novel postbiotic supplement containing inanimate bacterial strains may help the human body produce higher levels of its own GLP-1 hormone, potentially providing an affordable alternative to popular weight-loss medications like semaglutide. Early clinical research indicates that these specialized, heat-treated micro-organisms can effectively stimulate critical metabolic pathways in the gut without requiring live probiotic cultures to survive and colonize the digestive tract. This breakthrough opens up new avenues for non-prescription metabolic health interventions.

The small preliminary trial evaluated the targeted supplement's overall impact on metabolic health and weight management in human participants over a defined study period. Results demonstrated a measurable increase in natural GLP-1 hormone secretion, which was accompanied by noticeable improvements in daily appetite control, reduced cravings, and gradual reductions in body weight. Researchers emphasize that because postbiotics are highly stable at room temperature and far easier to manufacture than living bacteria, they could significantly reduce production expenses and expand global accessibility.

Despite these encouraging initial findings, medical experts caution that larger, long-term clinical trials are essential to thoroughly evaluate the supplement's efficacy, optimal dosage, and long-term safety profile. While the supplement may not achieve the same rapid or dramatic weight loss seen with high-dose prescription injectables, researchers suggest this approach could offer a safe, accessible, and sustainable tool for individuals seeking manageable metabolic support and long-term weight management.
