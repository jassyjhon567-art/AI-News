Israeli Ad Tech Firm Discover-Tech Used as Conduit for Candiru's Zero-Click Spyware

www.securityweek.com · Sat, 17 Oct 2026 11:00:00 +0000

An Israeli advertising firm reportedly enabled spyware vendor Candiru to fingerprint devices and deploy zero-click surveillance malware to targeted individuals worldwide via commercial ads.

An Israeli digital advertising company known as Discover-Tech reportedly functioned as a delivery channel for Candiru, a prominent mercenary spyware vendor. Recent investigations indicate that the firm's commercial ad-serving infrastructure provided cyber adversaries with the capability to fingerprint and track devices across the internet at an unprecedented scale, effectively laying the groundwork for targeted cyber surveillance operations.

According to findings in the cybersecurity community, the advertising network was weaponized to selectively infect individuals with zero-click exploits. By embedding tracking mechanisms inside legitimate-looking online advertisements, the operators could assess a target's operating system, browser, and hardware profile. Once an individual of interest was identified, Candiru's spyware could be silently deployed onto the device without requiring the victim to click on any suspicious link or download untrusted files.

This development underscores a concerning convergence between the commercial adtech industry and offensive cyber intelligence operations. Utilizing established ad networks allows spyware developers to exploit extensive global distribution channels while maintaining plausible deniability. The revelation has heightened alarm among security analysts and privacy advocates, who caution that the weaponization of online advertising poses a pervasive threat to journalists, activists, and civil society worldwide.
