Abby Care Secures $82 Million to Address Caregiver Shortage Through Unconventional Approach

www.inc.com · Sun, 18 Oct 2026 22:26:30 -0400

Abby Care has secured $82 million in Series C funding to tackle the caregiver shortage by training and compensating family members to provide in-home care.

Abby Care has officially announced the successful closure of an $82 million Series C funding round designed to radically transform the home healthcare ecosystem. The innovative startup seeks to directly confront the persistent and growing shortage of professional caregivers by rethinking how long-term in-home care is delivered, structured, and sustained. Rather than merely competing for the severely limited pool of certified medical professionals in a depleted labor market, the company is championing a distinct model that empowers family members to become formally compensated, highly supported caregivers.

The deepening shortage of registered nurses and certified nursing assistants has placed an unsustainable burden on domestic healthcare systems, leaving millions of vulnerable patients without reliable personal care services. Abby Care addresses this crisis by providing a specialized platform that navigates administrative hurdles and supplies relatives with the practical training, financial compensation, and operational tools required to serve as formal home care providers. By formally embedding family members into the paid care continuum, the startup effectively bypasses conventional staffing bottlenecks while ensuring that patients receive compassionate, personalized care from people they already trust.

With this substantial capital infusion, Abby Care plans to expand its operational footprint across new regions, enhance its proprietary technology platform, and expand support services for enrolled families. Healthcare analysts view this innovative approach as a vital step forward, particularly as aging demographics worldwide create unprecedented demand for long-term domestic support. By legitimizing informal caregiving and integrating non-traditional providers into the healthcare ecosystem, Abby Care aims to deliver a scalable, sustainable solution to one of the most pressing labor challenges of our time.
