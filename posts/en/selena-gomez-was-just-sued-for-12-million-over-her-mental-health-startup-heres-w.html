Selena Gomez Sued for $1.2 Million by Investors Over Mental Health Startup Commitments

www.entrepreneur.com · Fri, 14 Aug 2026 18:43:00 GMT

Investors have filed a $1.2 million lawsuit against Selena Gomez, alleging the star breached commitments to actively promote and support her mental health startup.

Global pop icon and actress Selena Gomez is facing a new legal challenge after investors filed a $1.2 million lawsuit regarding her involvement in a mental health startup venture. The legal action, lodged in civil court, centers on allegations that the celebrated artist and entrepreneur failed to follow through on critical promotional and operational commitments that were fundamental to the company's founding agreements and ongoing financial strategy.

According to details outlined in the complaint, the plaintiffs allege that their substantial capital contributions were directly tied to Gomez’s promise to actively utilize her massive global reach—particularly as the most-followed woman on Instagram—to drive public awareness, user acquisition, and commercial partnerships for the platform. However, the investors claim that she systematically neglected her agreed-upon responsibilities, failing to participate in scheduled marketing campaigns, media appearances, and key executive engagements. The filing asserts that this lack of direct participation severely stalled the startup's market momentum, diminished its projected valuation, and caused significant financial harm to the backing partners.

The lawsuit highlights the increasingly complex dynamics surrounding celebrity-backed consumer brands and wellness startups, where financial success is heavily dependent on the sustained personal endorsement and star power of high-profile founders. As legal proceedings commence, representatives for Gomez have yet to publicly respond in detail to the specific claims presented in the suit. The case is now set to test the boundaries of contractual obligations and celebrity accountability in modern venture capital partnerships.
