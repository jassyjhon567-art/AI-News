Indulgent Frozen Mississippi Mud Pie: The Ultimate Coffee and Bourbon Dessert

www.bonappetit.com · Fri, 24 Jul 2026 17:50:16 +0000

This indulgent frozen Mississippi Mud Pie combines a crunchy Oreo crust, rich coffee ice cream, velvety bourbon fudge, and toasted pecans for a sophisticated celebration dessert.

The iconic Mississippi Mud Pie receives a sophisticated gourmet twist with this decadent frozen coffee ice cream rendition. Celebrated for its rich chocolate profile and delightful textures, this classic Southern dessert is elevated into an unforgettable frozen treat. Built upon a dark, crunchy crust made from crushed Oreo cookies, the pie effortlessly combines the bold, roasted flavor of premium coffee with rich, velvety ice cream, creating an irresistible treat for both coffee and chocolate lovers.

At the heart of this frozen masterpiece lies a carefully crafted arrangement of complementary layers and rich flavors. Smooth coffee ice cream forms the substantial center filling, which is layered with a luxurious bourbon-infused chocolate fudge sauce. The subtle warmth and woody notes of the bourbon cut through the sweet chocolate, adding a refined depth to the dish. To complete the creation, a generous topping of toasted pecans is scattered across the surface, delivering a delightful nuttiness and a satisfying crunch with every bite.

Beyond its spectacular taste, this frozen Mississippi Mud Pie is an ideal dessert for hosting since it can be fully prepared well in advance of any dinner party or family gathering. Allowing the pie to set in the freezer ensures clean slices and a perfectly chilled texture upon serving. Whether presented at a festive summer barbecue or a cozy holiday dinner, this elegant dessert offers a memorable fusion of coffee, bourbon fudge, and toasted pecans that is guaranteed to impress your guests.
