TextNow Launches Kid-Friendly Moto G Play Smartphone with $20 Monthly Plan

www.cnet.com · 2026-10-20T13:00:00Z

TextNow has introduced a kid-friendly Moto G Play for $150 with a $20 monthly plan, featuring a parent portal for tracking location and managing communications.

TextNow has officially introduced a customized version of the Motorola Moto G Play designed specifically for children. Priced at an accessible $150, the smartphone aims to provide parents with a safe, manageable, and cost-effective entry point for introducing their kids to mobile technology as they grow older. To ensure ongoing expenses remain predictable and budget-friendly, the carrier pairs the device with a dedicated $20 monthly service plan, offering families an all-in-one connectivity solution without the financial burden of costly flagship devices.

Central to the device’s appeal is a comprehensive parent portal that gives caregivers extensive oversight over their child's digital interactions. Through this intuitive management dashboard, parents can approve and regulate incoming and outgoing phone calls, manage text messaging privileges, and monitor the smartphone’s physical location in real time. In addition, the portal allows guardians to supervise and selectively enable games and apps, helping families establish healthy screen-time habits while keeping unwanted digital distractions at bay.

By integrating these parental controls directly into an economical package, TextNow seeks to address common concerns surrounding mobile safety, privacy, and online exposure among younger audiences. The pairing of a $150 hardware purchase and a modest $20 monthly subscription offers a practical, low-risk alternative to giving children unrestricted mobile access without requiring separate parental software. It strikes a pragmatic balance, providing youngsters with essential communication tools while granting parents the continuous supervision and peace of mind they require.
