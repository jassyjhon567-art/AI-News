Airlines Waive Change Fees Across Northeast as Severe Nor'easter Knocks Out Power

www.businessinsider.com · 2026-09-26T14:42:23Z

Major airlines have waived change fees as a severe nor'easter battered the northeastern United States, knocking out power to over 88,000 customers and causing extensive travel disruptions.

A powerful and rapidly intensifying nor'easter swept across the northeastern United States on Saturday, delivering intense snowfall, fierce coastal winds, and severe disruptions to regional transportation networks. In response to the hazardous winter weather, major domestic airlines moved quickly to issue travel advisories and waive ticket change fees. These waivers allow affected passengers traveling to, from, or through major airports in the region to adjust their itineraries without facing standard penalties or fare adjustments.

The impacts of the winter storm extended far beyond aviation, causing widespread damage to public infrastructure and leaving communities in the dark. Heavy, moisture-laden snow combined with damaging wind gusts brought down trees and utility lines, leaving more than 88,000 customers without power by Saturday morning. Emergency management officials reported scattered outages stretching across multiple coastal and inland areas, while utility companies mobilized additional repair crews to restore electricity under freezing and challenging field conditions.

Roadways across the affected states became increasingly dangerous as blowing snow caused whiteout conditions and severe icy patches. State transportation departments and local emergency officials strongly urged motorists to stay off the roads to allow plows and emergency vehicles to operate safely. With severe weather conditions anticipated to persist through the weekend, travelers and residents alike were advised to remain vigilant, keep emergency supplies handy, and continuously monitor flight schedules and local forecasts.
