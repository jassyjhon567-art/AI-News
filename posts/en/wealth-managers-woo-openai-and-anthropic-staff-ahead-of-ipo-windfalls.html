Wealth Managers Court OpenAI and Anthropic Employees Ahead of Potential IPO Windfalls

www.ft.com · Thu, 13 Aug 2026 04:00:04 GMT

Wealth managers are aggressively competing for equity-rich employees at OpenAI and Anthropic ahead of anticipated IPO windfalls, shifting negotiating power toward these tech clients.

Wealth management firms and private banks are aggressively competing to win over employees at leading artificial intelligence startups, including OpenAI and Anthropic. With both companies experiencing soaring valuations, early staff members and senior engineers are poised for massive financial windfalls from potential initial public offerings (IPOs) or private secondary share sales. Consequently, financial advisers are eager to establish early relationships with this newly minted demographic.

This influx of prospective paper wealth has fundamentally shifted the dynamic between financial advisers and tech professionals. Tech workers holding lucrative equity stakes now command greater bargaining power, demanding specialized services, lower management fees, and bespoke tax strategies. Wealth managers are being forced to adapt, offering sophisticated estate planning, concentrated stock hedging, and liquidity management solutions tailored to the unique financial complexities faced by startup employees.

The intense race to court AI talent highlights a broader transformation within the wealth management industry. As artificial intelligence continues to drive the next wave of Silicon Valley prosperity, financial institutions recognize that securing these clients early can guarantee long-term assets under management. These newly wealthy individuals are reshaping wealth advisory standards, prioritizing tech-savvy, flexible, and highly personalized financial guidance over traditional banking relationships.
