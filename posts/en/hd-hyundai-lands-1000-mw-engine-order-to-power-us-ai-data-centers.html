HD Hyundai Secures $673.8 Million Power Deal for U.S. AI Data Centers

www.unite.ai · Mon, 10 Aug 2026 03:23:04 +0000

HD Hyundai has signed a $673.8 million contract to supply 1,000 megawatts of HiMSEN engine power systems to Corban Energy Group for U.S. AI data centers.

HD Hyundai Heavy Industries has secured a major energy deal valued at $673.8 million to supply 1,000 megawatts of power generation systems designed for data centers across the United States. Announced on August 9, 2026, the agreement highlights the soaring electricity demand fueled by the rapid expansion of artificial intelligence applications and modern cloud computing services. Under the terms of the contract, the South Korean manufacturer will deliver its advanced equipment to Corban Energy Group, a prominent American energy infrastructure developer.

The supplied equipment will directly feed data centers operated by a leading U.S. technology company, providing reliable electricity in regions where local grid infrastructure faces capacity constraints. At the core of the power generation setup is HD Hyundai’s proprietary 9.6-megawatt HiMSEN engine. Known for its high performance and reliability, this medium-speed engine is engineered specifically for continuous, large-scale power generation, making it an ideal choice for sustaining mission-critical server facilities without interruption.

This significant supply deal underscores a broader trend among major technology firms seeking independent, high-capacity power solutions to support next-generation computing workloads. As artificial intelligence models demand unprecedented levels of energy, partnerships between global heavy industry leaders and power infrastructure developers are becoming vital to sustaining technological growth. The contract represents a major commercial achievement for HD Hyundai as it expands its footprint within the North American energy equipment market.
