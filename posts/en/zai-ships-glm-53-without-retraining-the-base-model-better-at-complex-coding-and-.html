Z.ai Unveils GLM-5.3: Advancing Complex Coding and Agentic Tasks via Scaled Post-Training

www.marktechpost.com · Fri, 14 Aug 2026 08:03:22 +0000

Z.ai has launched GLM-5.3, utilizing scaled post-training on its existing 743B base model to achieve significant performance breakthroughs in software engineering, terminal execution, and cybersecurity.

Artificial intelligence lab Z.ai has officially announced the launch of GLM-5.3, marking a significant milestone in AI reasoning and multi-step task execution. Notably, the new model keeps the original 743-billion-parameter GLM-5.2 base architecture completely unchanged, bypassing the computational expense of full base-model retraining. Instead, all reported performance improvements stem entirely from scaled post-training methods, which incorporated expanded environment types, long-horizon task scenarios, and longer training durations.

The model demonstrates substantial performance leaps across demanding software engineering and command-line execution benchmarks. On Terminal-Bench 3.0, GLM-5.3 surged from an earlier baseline score of 4.6 to 28.3, while its DeepSWE v1.1 score advanced from 46.2 to 66.9. The performance gains were even more pronounced in cybersecurity—exceeding internal projections—with the model achieving an 84.5% score on CyberGym and more than doubling its previous results on ExploitBench to reach 54.4%.

This release highlights the transformative potential of sophisticated post-training methodologies in expanding the frontier of complex problem-solving without starting from scratch. By enabling models to navigate rich, interactive environments over extended horizons, developers can unlock higher-order agentic capabilities for technical workloads. Z.ai stated that the open model weights for GLM-5.3 will be released to the public in approximately two weeks.
