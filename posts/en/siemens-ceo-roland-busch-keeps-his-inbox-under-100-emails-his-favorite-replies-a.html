Siemens CEO Roland Busch Shares His Minimalist Approach to Managing Email and Meetings

www.inc.com · Wed, 19 Aug 2026 13:30:00 -0400

Siemens CEO Roland Busch boosts productivity by maintaining a lean inbox, using one-word email replies, and eliminating recurring one-on-one meetings.

In an era where corporate leaders are constantly inundated with digital communications, Siemens Chief Executive Roland Busch maintains strict control over his daily workflow. Busch actively keeps his inbox count under 100 messages, relying on extreme brevity to process incoming communications efficiently. His most frequent responses are often reduced to a single word, such as "OK" or "No," allowing him to make swift decisions and prevent administrative backlog.

Beyond simplifying written communications, Busch has also redesigned his approach to corporate scheduling. The chief executive avoids routine, recurring one-on-one meetings with team members, arguing that scheduled check-ins often waste valuable time when there are no pressing issues to address. Instead, he favors spontaneous, need-based interactions that focus exclusively on immediate strategic priorities, fostering greater operational agility within the executive leadership structure.

Busch's minimalist management style reflects a broader trend among modern executives who are actively trimming workplace inefficiencies. As digital fatigue and meeting overload continue to hinder productivity across global enterprises, business leaders are increasingly stripping away bureaucratic communication habits in favor of direct, action-oriented methods designed to keep teams focused on core corporate goals.
