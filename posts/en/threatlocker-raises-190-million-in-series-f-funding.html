ThreatLocker Secures $190 Million in Series F Funding to Drive Global Expansion

www.securityweek.com · Wed, 29 Jul 2026 12:17:14 +0000

Cybersecurity firm ThreatLocker has raised $190 million in Series F funding, significantly boosting its prior $1.6 billion valuation to expand its zero-trust endpoint protection platform.

Leading cybersecurity enterprise ThreatLocker has officially secured $190 million in its Series F funding round, reflecting sustained momentum and robust investor confidence in the platform's security capabilities. Prior to this latest financial milestone, the company had attained a valuation of $1.6 billion. The recent influx of capital has significantly elevated that valuation, reinforcing ThreatLocker's status as a dominant player in the rapidly evolving endpoint security ecosystem.

The newly raised capital will be utilized to accelerate operational growth, expand international market reach, and bolster continuous research and development efforts. ThreatLocker's core offerings center on zero-trust endpoint protection, incorporating strict application control and ringfencing technologies designed to block unauthorized software execution. As modern enterprises face an escalating volume of ransomware attacks and data breaches, the demand for robust, proactive defense mechanisms has grown exponentially across various industry sectors.

By prioritizing a default-deny approach to cybersecurity, ThreatLocker has successfully established a distinct position within the marketplace, serving numerous managed service providers and corporate clients globally. The substantial financial support secured in this Series F round ensures that the organization remains well-equipped to innovate its product offerings, scale its service infrastructure, and navigate the increasingly complex threat landscape confronting modern digital organizations worldwide.
