Celebrate National Spritz Day with Refreshing Cocktails and Nation-Wide Deals

www.fastcompany.com · 2026-08-01T11:01:00

National Spritz Day on August 1 brings nationwide discounts and celebrations across America, honoring the classic Italian cocktail's rich history and diverse modern variations.

National Spritz Day, celebrated annually on August 1, offers cocktail enthusiasts a refreshing escape without needing an expensive European vacation. The popular drink evolved in northern Italy's Veneto region during the 19th century when Austro-Hungarian soldiers diluted strong local wines with a splash or "spritz" of water. Over time, the recipe matured into the classic formula widely enjoyed today: three parts prosecco, two parts bitter Italian aperitif, and one part soda water served over ice. While the ubiquitous Aperol Spritz remains the most recognized version, modern variations feature liqueurs like Campari, limoncello, and elderflower syrup.

Establishments across the United States are marking the occasion with discounted offerings and unique celebrations. In the West, Tucson's Bacio is serving discounted personal spritzes alongside food purchases, while Flight Club in Las Vegas offers $12 options alongside interactive dart games. Heading South, Austin's Stargazer Bar features affordable traditional and specialty rose spritzes. Meanwhile, The Everly in Madison, Wisconsin, extends the festivities with a week-long celebration showcasing various flavored and alcohol-free options.

On the East Coast, New York City presents multiple venues for festive imbibers, including discounted spritzes paired with shuffleboard at Electric Shuffle and evening specials at Brooklyn's Time Out Market. Whether featuring traditional Aperol or novel zero-proof combinations, these nationwide promotions allow patrons to partake in the vibrant Italian tradition locally. Ultimately, National Spritz Day highlights how historical regional beverage traditions can transform into popular global culture and accessible summer celebrations.
