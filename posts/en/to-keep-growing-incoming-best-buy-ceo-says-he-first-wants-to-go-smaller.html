Incoming Best Buy CEO Jason Bonfig Strategy Focuses on Smaller Stores to Drive Growth

www.cnbc.com · Sat, 01 Aug 2026 12:00:01 GMT

Incoming Best Buy CEO Jason Bonfig plans to drive company growth by opening smaller store formats to improve market reach and enhance the overall customer shopping experience.

As Jason Bonfig prepares to step into the role of Chief Executive Officer at Best Buy, he is outlining a clear and strategic vision that focuses on scaling down physical store formats to unlock long-term business growth. Speaking in a recent interview with CNBC, the incoming leader emphasized that opening smaller retail outlets will allow the consumer electronics giant to expand its geographic reach and enter vibrant local markets where traditional large-format big-box stores are simply not practical or economically feasible, as well as drive sustained revenue streams across new customer segments.

Bonfig explained that this strategic pivot toward smaller footprints is specifically designed to elevate the overall customer experience by making shopping more convenient, local, and efficient. By seamlessly blending these compact physical locations with robust digital and omnichannel services, Best Buy aims to adapt to changing consumer preferences for rapid order pickup, curated product selections, and personalized expert consultation, which continues to be a cornerstone of the company's identity.

The move comes at a crucial time for major brick-and-mortar retailers navigating changing economic conditions and shifted shopping patterns. By placing an emphasis on smaller retail concepts, Best Buy expects to optimize inventory management, reduce substantial real estate overhead, and build a more agile store network across key demographic regions, allowing the firm to quickly adapt to market trends. Bonfig expressed confidence that this refined strategy will foster customer loyalty and firmly position the company for sustainable financial performance in a competitive retail landscape.
