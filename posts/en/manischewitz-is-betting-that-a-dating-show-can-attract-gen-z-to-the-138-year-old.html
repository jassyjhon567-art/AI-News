Manischewitz Turns to Dating Shows and Modern Rebranding to Woo Gen Z

www.fastcompany.com · 2026-09-12T10:00:00

Heritage kosher brand Manischewitz is successfully attracting Gen Z consumers through an extensive revamp featuring a dating show, trendy merchandise, and convenient frozen foods.

Founded in 1888, the 138-year-old kosher food brand Manischewitz has long been a cherished staple of Jewish holiday celebrations, known for traditional items such as wine, matzo, and gefilte fish during sacred observances like Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur. However, as cultural habits shift across generations, the heritage company is actively seeking to engage younger demographics, particularly Gen Z consumers whose cultural and religious expressions differ noticeably from those of their parents and grandparents. Rather than expecting youth to adopt legacy traditions directly, leadership is actively reshaping how the brand intersects with modern culture.

Following an ambitious brand refresh in 2024, the company expanded well beyond standard packaging updates. To capture the attention of culturally curious younger audiences, Manischewitz launched an original dating show titled "Manischewitz Matchmakers," debuted a traveling deli food truck serving hot knishes and matzo ball soup, and developed vibrant lifestyle merchandise alongside updated digital content. The brand has also widened its portfolio to include gluten-free, low-sodium, and sugar-free selections, ensuring traditional Jewish food products remain accessible, appealing, and relevant to modern dietary preferences.

This lifestyle-driven marketing strategy is already yielding significant commercial gains. Since its 2024 modernization, the brand has reported overall sales growth exceeding 12%, propelled by a 40% surge in core matzo products largely attributed to younger buyers. Furthermore, sales in the brand's convenient fresh and frozen segment have tripled across major metropolitan markets, driven by ready-to-heat items such as frozen challah and matzo balls. By harmonizing century-old authenticity with contemporary engagement, Manischewitz is forging lasting connections across generations.
