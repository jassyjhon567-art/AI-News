Philippines Needs 'Power of All Sorts' for Energy Security After Nationwide Crisis, Says Prime Infra CEO

fortune.com · Thu, 13 Aug 2026 04:30:00 +0000

Following a nationwide crisis, Prime Infra CEO Guillaume Lucci emphasized that the Philippines needs a diverse mix of energy sources to achieve reliable, long-term energy security.

In the wake of a severe nationwide power crisis, the Philippines is facing urgent calls to revamp its energy infrastructure to ensure sustainable and reliable power generation. Speaking in an interview at the company's headquarters in Pasay City, Manila, Prime Infra Chief Executive Officer Guillaume Lucci highlighted that the country requires a diverse approach to energy development. Lucci emphasized that securing the nation's energy future will demand power generation from every available source rather than relying exclusively on green initiatives.

"What we need is more energy of all sorts, not only more renewable energy," Lucci stated during his interview with Fortune. While transitioning toward cleaner energy sources remains a global priority, the immediate priority for the Philippines is to eliminate power deficits that disrupt daily life and economic growth. Integrating traditional power resources alongside emerging renewable technologies like solar and wind provides a realistic roadmap to satisfy rising consumption levels, stabilize the national grid, and prevent widespread blackouts.

As a major infrastructure developer, Prime Infra is actively working to address these critical energy challenges through diverse investments across the country. Lucci’s perspective reflects a growing consensus among industrial leaders that energy security requires a pragmatic balance between sustainability and continuous baseline supply. By bolstering power generation capacity through multi-faceted energy projects, the Philippines aims to create a more resilient power grid capable of driving long-term economic development.
