Chinese Chipmaker CXMT Surges 466% in Record-Breaking Market Debut

www.ft.com · Mon, 27 Jul 2026 07:29:15 GMT

Chinese semiconductor producer CXMT saw its shares skyrocket by 466 percent during mainland China's largest initial public offering since 2010, briefly making it the region's most valuable listed company.

Chinese memory semiconductor champion ChangXin Memory Technologies (CXMT) witnessed a spectacular market debut as its stock price surged by an astonishing 466 percent during its first day of trading. The landmark transaction represents mainland China’s largest initial public offering since 2010, generating immense interest from both retail and institutional investors. The unprecedented opening rally briefly propelled the semiconductor group to become the most valuable listed enterprise across mainland stock exchanges, setting a major milestone for the domestic financial landscape.

The record-breaking public listing highlights the overwhelming enthusiasm among domestic investors for home-grown technology firms amid evolving global trade dynamics. As international pressure and supply chain constraints continue to push China toward greater self-reliance in vital high-tech sectors, local chip manufacturers have acquired paramount strategic importance. CXMT’s successful public entry reflects powerful market confidence in the nation's sustained drive to establish advanced memory chip design and production capabilities independent of foreign technology suppliers.

Beyond the immediate trading frenzy, the massive market debut signals a broader resurgence of capital deployment in mainland equity markets, specifically targeted toward key technology and industrial manufacturing sectors. Strong capital inflows into semiconductor enterprises demonstrate how aligned private and institutional investments have become with broader economic modernization goals. As market analysts closely observe the stock's post-debut performance and long-term stability, this historic listing underscores the expanding financial weight of domestic technology leaders in driving future industrial growth.
