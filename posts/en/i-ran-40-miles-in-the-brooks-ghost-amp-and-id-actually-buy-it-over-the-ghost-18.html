Brooks Ghost Amp Review: A More Energetic Alternative to the Ghost 18

www.tomsguide.com · Sat, 17 Oct 2026 09:30:00 +0000

The Brooks Ghost Amp offers a livelier, more responsive alternative to the Ghost 18, delivering an energetic daily running experience despite facing strong competition from rival brands.

The Brooks Ghost series has long held a reputation as a dependable staple in the running world, celebrated for its balanced cushioning, reliability, and everyday comfort. With the release of the Brooks Ghost Amp, the brand introduces a notably livelier iteration designed to sit alongside the traditional Ghost 18. Extensive road testing over forty miles demonstrates that this model successfully reinvents the classic daily trainer experience, offering an energetic underfoot feel that caters to runners looking for greater propulsion during their routine runs.

When compared directly to the Ghost 18, the Ghost Amp stands out primarily through its distinct responsiveness. While the standard Ghost remains a steadfast option for slow recovery efforts, the Amp features a springier foam composition that delivers enhanced energy return and promotes smoother, faster transitions. The upper remains plush and breathable, retaining the secure lockdown Brooks runners expect. For anyone who found previous iterations of the Ghost line slightly muted or flat, the Ghost Amp provides an engaging upgrade that makes it a more compelling daily purchase than its sibling.

However, this lively addition faces serious challenges in an increasingly competitive footwear landscape. Rival manufacturers have spent recent years packing innovative super-critical foams and modern geometry into their own daily training lines. Consequently, the Ghost Amp must contend with exceptional alternatives that offer similar, if not superior, weight and bounce. Even so, the shoe represents a welcome and necessary evolution for the Brooks lineup, providing brand loyalists and everyday athletes with a genuinely fun and versatile trainer.
