Gen Z Women Lead Entrepreneurship Surge Driven by Need for Flexibility and Autonomy

www.fastcompany.com · 2026-07-30T07:00:00

A recent survey reveals that Gen Z women are leading a surge in female entrepreneurship, driven by flexibility needs, parenting responsibilities, and lower reliance on artificial intelligence.

A new survey from Gusto highlights a significant shift in business creation, with Gen Z women emerging as the primary drivers of female entrepreneurship. In 2025, 47% of new female-owned businesses were launched by Gen Z women, up from 38% the previous year. This surge places young women ahead of older generations, including Gen X, Millennials, and Baby Boomers. Additionally, Black women played a pivotal role in this growth, founding 69% of all new Black-owned businesses established during the year.

Parenting and rising childcare costs have significantly contributed to this entrepreneurial wave. Nearly 20% of Gen Z women starting businesses in 2025 were parents, compared to just 11% of their male counterparts. Facing skyrocketing childcare expenses that recently forced hundreds of thousands of women out of the traditional workforce, many mothers are turning to business ownership for schedule flexibility and economic independence. In fact, 15% of female business owners cited parenting as a primary motivation, compared to a mere 1% of men.

Interestingly, the research reveals that young female entrepreneurs rely far less on artificial intelligence to start their ventures compared to men. Only 2.4% of Gen Z women stated they needed AI access to launch their business, whereas 16.2% of Gen Z men considered AI crucial to their startup decisions. Despite lower tech dependency, Gen Z women are demonstrating resilience and determination, carving out new pathways to professional success on their own terms.
