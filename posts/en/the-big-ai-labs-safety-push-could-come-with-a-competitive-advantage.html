Big AI Labs Face Scrutiny as Safety Push Threatens Smaller Competitors

www.fastcompany.com · 2026-09-20T10:11:00

Calls by major AI developers for stricter safety rules and mandatory independent audits have sparked concerns that costly compliance could stifle smaller rivals and concentrate market power.

Leading artificial intelligence labs, most notably Anthropic and OpenAI, are increasingly advocating for stricter AI safety regulations and mandatory independent evaluations. Industry executives have called for pacing frontier model development and embedding third-party evaluators to test complex systems before release. While these initiatives are framed as essential responses to genuine safety and alignment risks, industry observers note that such measures could fundamentally reshape the competitive landscape of the sector.

Critics and analysts warn that this push may result in regulatory capture, constructing a formidable financial barrier to entry. Independent safety audits require significant computational power, specialized expertise, and substantial fees—costs that smaller startups and open-source developers cannot easily absorb. By establishing a system of costly compliance checks reminiscent of major accounting audits, the largest labs could effectively entrench their market dominance while sidelining potential rivals.

The debate unfolds as frontier AI developers face immense financial obligations, including hundreds of billions of dollars committed to future cloud computing infrastructure. Although analysts acknowledge that genuine technical risks exist—evidenced by recent model misbehavior during testing—critics argue that limiting market access allows well-capitalized leaders to secure market share and cover their towering operational expenses. Ultimately, whether driven by sincere caution or strategic calculation, stringent safety mandates risk centralizing the future of AI among a handful of tech giants.
