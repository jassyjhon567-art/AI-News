Platform Engineering Maturity Key to Enterprise AI Success, Perforce Report Finds

www.infoq.com · Tue, 04 Aug 2026 12:00:00 GMT

Perforce Software's 2026 report reveals that platform engineering maturity is vital for enterprise success in transforming AI investments into sustainable operational value.

The newly released 2026 Platform Engineering Report from Perforce Software highlights a fundamental shift in how modern enterprises approach artificial intelligence adoption. According to the report, platform engineering maturity has emerged as a defining factor in determining whether organizations can successfully convert initial AI initiatives into long-term, sustainable operational value. As businesses worldwide accelerate their investments in machine learning and generative AI technologies, robust platform infrastructure is proving indispensable for managing architectural complexity, ensuring system stability, and maintaining fast software delivery across all organizational levels.

Organizations with mature platform engineering practices are significantly better equipped to overcome the operational bottlenecks that frequently hinder enterprise-wide AI integration. By offering standardized internal developer platforms, automated software pipelines, and self-service capabilities, mature infrastructure teams effectively eliminate developer friction. This structured environment allows engineering teams to build, deploy, and monitor AI-driven applications seamlessly, ensuring that corporate governance, data security, and performance standards are rigorously maintained across cloud ecosystems without slowing down the pace of ongoing innovation.

Ultimately, the research underscores that simply acquiring cutting-edge AI capabilities is insufficient to guarantee long-term business success. Without a mature platform engineering foundation, organizations risk encountering workflow fragmentation, operational inefficiencies, and escalating technical debt. Consequently, enterprise leaders are increasingly treating platform maturity not merely as an internal IT objective, but as a crucial strategic imperative necessary to maximize return on investment, optimize developer productivity, and secure a sustainable competitive advantage in an increasingly competitive digital AI landscape.
