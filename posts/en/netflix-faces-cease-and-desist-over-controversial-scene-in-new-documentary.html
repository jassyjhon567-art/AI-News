Netflix Hit With Cease-and-Desist Letter Over Controversial Scene in Prediction Market Documentary

www.fastcompany.com · 2026-07-27T20:45:00

Prediction market platform Kalshi has sent a cease-and-desist letter to Netflix, alleging a documentary trailer misleadingly portrays an illegal bet in Nevada.

Streaming giant Netflix is facing legal pressure after receiving a cease-and-desist letter from prediction market platform Kalshi over a controversial scene in a trailer for its upcoming documentary, Instadocs: The Prediction Games. The film explores the rapidly growing prediction market industry and highlights prominent platforms like Kalshi and Polymarket. Controversy erupted over footage of a World Cup watch party in Las Vegas, where an attendee boasts about placing a $5,000 wager on Kalshi regarding whether Spain would win the 2026 FIFA World Cup.

Kalshi has labeled the trailer defamatory and misleading, emphasizing that sports betting on its platform is currently banned in the state of Nevada. The company clarified that the wager shown on screen was actually a legitimate bet placed in May 2025, well before the Nevada ban went into effect. Kalshi asserted that a Netflix employee had previously promised to exclude the receipt from the documentary, yet the streaming service inexplicably published it anyway in the trailer currently featured on the Netflix homepage.

Despite the legal warning, Netflix stands firmly by its documentary footage, denying that any material was fabricated or altered. A Netflix spokesperson explained that the scenes were filmed during a live event in Las Vegas in July 2026, where the featured trader voluntarily shared a screenshot of his prior bet. The streaming service added that any transactions shown reflect actions taken strictly between individual users and the platforms they utilize, maintaining that the trailer remains an accurate representation of the event.
