SpaceX Could Reach $500 Billion in Revenue by 2028 Through AI Expansion, Elon Musk Says

www.space.com · Tue, 11 Aug 2026 23:42:48 +0000

Elon Musk claims SpaceX is evolving from a launch provider into an artificial intelligence outfit, potentially driving its annual revenue to $500 billion by 2028.

Space Exploration Technologies Corp., widely known as SpaceX, is undergoing a monumental strategic evolution that extends far beyond its original mandate as a space transportation provider. According to chief executive Elon Musk, the private aerospace enterprise is rapidly transforming into a major player within the global artificial intelligence sector. Musk indicated that this ambitious pivot toward advanced computing infrastructure, automated systems, and machine learning technology could position the company to generate an extraordinary $500 billion in annual revenue by the year 2028.

Originally established to revolutionize space travel and drastically reduce launch costs, SpaceX initially built its global reputation through reusable orbital rocket technology and the rapid expansion of its Starlink satellite constellation. However, the complex operational demands of managing massive satellite networks, real-time spatial navigation, and autonomous spacecraft hardware have naturally pushed the company deeper into artificial intelligence development. Musk believes that seamlessly blending global satellite infrastructure with cutting-edge AI software capabilities will unlock unprecedented commercial opportunities and massive high-margin profit streams in the near future.

The lofty $500 billion financial projection underscores the growing market expectations for dual-purpose technology conglomerates capable of leveraging both hardware assets and sophisticated software algorithms. While reaching such an immense financial output within a few short years presents formidable operational challenges, the company's aggressive technological shift signals a broader transformation in the commercial space industry. Investors and global industry analysts will be closely watching how SpaceX successfully balances its foundational aerospace ambitions with its emerging identity as a high-tech artificial intelligence powerhouse.
