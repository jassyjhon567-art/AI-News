Porsche Restoration Shop Owner Convicted in Multi-Million Dollar Vehicle Fraud Scheme

www.slashgear.com · Fri, 14 Aug 2026 16:45:00 +0000

A Porsche restoration shop owner has been convicted of a ten-million-dollar fraud scheme involving the unauthorized sale of clients' rare classic vehicles.

The owner of a renowned luxury vehicle restoration shop has been convicted by a court for orchestrating a multi-million-dollar fraud scheme that victimized classic sports car enthusiasts. The business, which gained a reputation for specializing in the meticulous preservation and repair of vintage Porsche models, turned out to be the operational front for an elaborate illicit enterprise where clients were systematically deceived regarding the condition and whereabouts of their prized automobiles.

Court proceedings revealed that the shop owner accepted rare and highly valuable vintage sports cars from unsuspecting owners under the agreement of undertaking detailed, long-term restoration work. Instead of fulfilling these contracts, the owner covertly sold numerous client-owned vehicles to third-party buyers without authorization, pocketed millions of dollars in illicit profits, and falsified ownership documentation to cover up the sales. Over the course of several years, the total financial impact of the fraudulent activities surpassed ten million dollars.

Victims of the scheme were routinely kept in the dark for extended periods through misleading progress updates, fabricated technical excuses, and staged photographs meant to prove ongoing repairs. The guilty verdict marks a crucial step toward accountability for the affected automotive collectors whose trust and valuable property were exploited. The business owner now faces substantial prison time along with potential financial restitution orders as the case proceeds to final sentencing.
