Leadership Spotlight: Arun Hiremath on Driving Enterprise Automation and Growth at EvoluteIQ

www.unite.ai · Thu, 30 Jul 2026 17:03:09 +0000

Arun Hiremath, Chief Business Officer and Co-Founder of EvoluteIQ, leverages nearly three decades of technology expertise to drive the company’s enterprise hyperautomation growth and market expansion.

Arun Hiremath, the Chief Business Officer and Co-Founder of EvoluteIQ, is a highly accomplished technology executive and entrepreneur with nearly three decades of rich professional experience. Throughout his distinguished career in the global technology sector, Hiremath has accumulated deep expertise spanning enterprise automation, product strategy, business development, and telecommunications. His strategic leadership has played a pivotal role in helping multinational organizations navigate complex digital transformation journeys, enabling them to modernize legacy infrastructure and implement robust, forward-looking operational frameworks.

In his current leadership role at EvoluteIQ, Hiremath is primarily responsible for driving sustainable revenue growth and expanding the company’s operational presence globally. He leads strategic customer engagement initiatives, focusing specifically on scaling the growth of the company’s eiq360 business segment. Through this specialized offerings suite, Hiremath guides enterprises in leveraging integrated hyperautomation capabilities, allowing organizations to optimize intricate business processes, improve customer engagement, and significantly accelerate their digital application deployment timelines.

Before co-founding EvoluteIQ, Hiremath demonstrated a strong track record of entrepreneurial leadership, most notably as the co-founder and key executive of AmperAXP, an innovative low-code platform provider. His extensive background in developing low-code solutions and managing enterprise-grade product launches provides him with a nuanced understanding of software commercialization and market expansion. As hyperautomation continues to emerge as a critical priority for modern enterprises, Hiremath remains dedicated to delivering technological innovation and long-term business value to global clients.
