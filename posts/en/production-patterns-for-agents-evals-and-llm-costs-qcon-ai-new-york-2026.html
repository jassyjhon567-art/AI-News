QCon AI New York 2026 to Highlight Production Patterns for AI Agents, Evaluations, and LLM Costs

www.infoq.com · Tue, 13 Oct 2026 12:00:00 GMT

QCon AI New York 2026 will feature sessions from industry engineers addressing production challenges, including LLM token costs, evaluation pipelines, and security guardrails for autonomous agents.

Moving artificial intelligence and machine learning solutions from experimental research into live production environments introduces severe operational and architectural hurdles. As enterprises rapidly integrate large language models into core software systems, engineering teams encounter unprecedented scaling challenges. Code is generated and modified faster than peer review processes can accommodate, token consumption driving operational costs rises dramatically, and teams struggle to build automated evaluation frameworks that are sufficiently accurate and reliable to trigger automated production actions.

In addition to basic infrastructure demands, autonomous AI agents require sophisticated architectural safeguards, including strict permission models, execution limits, and behavioral guardrails to prevent harmful or unintended actions. To address these pressing concerns, the upcoming QCon AI New York 2026 conference, scheduled for December 15–16, will feature an extensive lineup of technical sessions led by experienced industry engineers. These practitioners will share practical frameworks and production patterns they have designed to govern complex AI workloads effectively.

Attendees at the conference will gain transparent insights into the real-world methods used to control inference expenditures, construct dependable automated evaluation pipelines, and isolate agentic capabilities securely. By prioritizing battle-tested engineering methodologies over speculative hype, the event aims to provide software architects, developers, and technical leaders with the concrete tools needed to operate robust, cost-effective, and safe generative AI systems at scale within modern enterprise environments.
