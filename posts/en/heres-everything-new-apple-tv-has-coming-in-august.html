Everything Coming to Apple TV+ This August: Returning Favorites and New Seasons

9to5mac.com · Mon, 03 Aug 2026 13:37:07 +0000

Apple TV+ announces its August lineup featuring the return of hit comedy Ted Lasso alongside new seasons and episodes of sci-fi series Dark Matter and Silo.

Apple TV+ is preparing for an action-packed month of high-profile entertainment this August, bringing subscribers an impressive and diverse lineup of returning fan favorites and fresh original programming. Leading the monthly slate is the long-awaited return of the critically acclaimed and award-winning comedy series Ted Lasso, which remains one of the streaming platform's biggest global hits. Fans who have been eagerly anticipating the next chapter in the heartwarming and uplifting story can look forward to new episodes rolling out this week, establishing a strong momentum for the service's late-summer broadcast schedule.

Alongside its premier comedy highlights, Apple TV+ is further strengthening its reputation as a primary destination for high-concept science fiction throughout the month of August. Viewers will be able to dive into a brand-new season of the mind-bending sci-fi thriller series Dark Matter, while the critically lauded dystopian drama Silo also makes its return with additional compelling episodes. Together, these prominent releases showcase the platform's ongoing commitment to delivering visually ambitious, high-budget, and narrative-rich speculative fiction to its growing international audience.

In addition to these marquee flagship titles, the August lineup features a broad selection of other returning original series and exclusive special releases scheduled to premiere across the coming weeks. Apple TV+ continues to execute its long-term strategy of prioritizing carefully curated, top-tier production values over sheer volume of content. As competition among major global streaming platforms remains exceptionally fierce, this comprehensive August showcase underscores Apple's sustained dedication to offering a compelling, high-quality, and diverse entertainment catalog.
