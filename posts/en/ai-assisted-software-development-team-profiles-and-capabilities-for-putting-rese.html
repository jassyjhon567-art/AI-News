AI-Assisted Software Development: Key Team Profiles and Capabilities from DORA's 2025 Research

www.infoq.com · Thu, 30 Jul 2026 11:30:00 GMT

DORA's 2025 research shows that maximizing AI in software development requires a strategic focus on organizational systems, providing actionable team profiles and capabilities for successful adoption.

Artificial intelligence serves as a powerful amplifier in modern software engineering, yet adopting new technology alone does not automatically guarantee superior results. Industry insights emphasize that a strategic focus on the overall organizational system yields the greatest returns. When software organizations integrate AI tools within a supportive operational culture, clear governance models, and well-defined workflows, engineering teams can maximize productivity, streamline complex software delivery cycles, and significantly reduce technical and friction points across the development lifecycle.

To assist teams in translating theoretical findings into tangible outcomes, DORA’s 2025 research on AI-assisted software development outlines distinct team profiles and success capabilities. This comprehensive study provides a structured framework for understanding how different engineering groups adopt and adapt to emerging intelligent tools. By categorizing team dynamics, skills, and daily practices, the research offers clear, actionable guidance for engineering leaders looking to modernize their software development processes based on proven empirical data.

Ultimately, successfully integrating AI into software development demands a holistic approach that aligns technological capabilities with organizational culture and process engineering. Companies that actively invest in optimizing their broader systems alongside technical tooling remain best positioned to achieve sustainable, long-term gains. By utilizing DORA’s team profiles and strategic capabilities, engineering organizations can foster an environment of continuous delivery, improved code quality, and enduring technical innovation.
