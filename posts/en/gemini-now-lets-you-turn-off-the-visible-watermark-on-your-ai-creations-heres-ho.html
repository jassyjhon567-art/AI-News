Google Gemini Allows Users to Disable Visible Watermarks on AI-Generated Media

www.techradar.com · Sun, 16 Aug 2026 12:30:00 +0000

Google Gemini now allows users to disable visible watermarks on AI-generated media while continuing to embed invisible digital signatures for transparency and authentication.

Google has introduced an update to its Gemini application, giving users the option to remove visible watermarks from AI-generated content. Creators generating images, videos, or audio through the platform can now choose whether an overt visual badge appears on their final output, offering cleaner results for personal and professional projects.

Users can easily adjust this feature within Gemini's settings menu. By navigating to the media generation preferences, individuals can toggle the visible watermark on or off according to their specific needs. This flexibility is expected to benefit digital artists, marketers, and content creators who require unblemished visuals for integration into broader multimedia workflows.

Despite the removal of visible branding, Google continues to address digital transparency and authentication. Content generated without a visible watermark will still carry invisible cryptographic markers, such as SynthID metadata. These embedded digital fingerprints allow platforms and detection tools to verify AI origins, helping mitigate misinformation while maintaining aesthetic quality for end users.
