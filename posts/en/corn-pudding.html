Transform Boxed Cornbread Mix Into an Extraordinary Cheddar Corn Pudding

www.bonappetit.com · Wed, 05 Aug 2026 19:34:58 +0000

Adding cheddar cheese, sour cream, and fresh chives easily transforms standard boxed cornbread mix into a rich, flavorful, and comforting corn pudding for any meal occasion.

Corn pudding has long held a special place on dining tables as a beloved comfort food, widely celebrated for its warm, soothing texture and gently sweet flavor profile. While preparing this classic southern side dish entirely from scratch can be a delightful culinary project, using a convenient box of pre-packaged cornbread mix offers a brilliant, time-saving shortcut for busy home cooks. By starting with this versatile and affordable pantry staple, anyone can craft a baked dish that feels remarkably lavish and entirely homemade without spending hours preparing ingredients in the kitchen.

The secret to transforming a modest store-bought baking mix into an extraordinary culinary centerpiece lies in a thoughtful combination of rich dairy products and vibrant fresh herbs. Incorporating generous amounts of sour cream ensures that the finished pudding retains a soft, custard-like moisture while imparting a light, appetizing tang to the batter. Meanwhile, sharp cheddar cheese contributes a bold, savory richness that perfectly balances the natural sweetness of the corn, while freshly chopped chives add a pop of bright color and a delicate aromatic lift.

Once baked until lightly golden on top and gently set in the center, this elevated corn pudding delivers a comforting dish that pairs seamlessly with a wide range of popular main courses. It serves as an exceptional addition to festive holiday banquets, summer barbecue spreads, or simple weeknight family suppers. By incorporating just a few accessible, high-impact additions, a basic cornbread mix is easily reinvented into an irresistible side dish that will consistently impress family and dinner guests alike.
