Induction Labs Introduces Photon-1: A Mixture-of-Experts Model Trained Entirely on Raw Video

www.marktechpost.com · Sun, 26 Jul 2026 09:14:22 +0000

Induction Labs has unveiled Photon-1, a 106-billion-parameter model that learns to simulate software, play checkers, and model physics by pretraining directly on raw video without action labels.

Traditional artificial intelligence agents that learn from visual media typically require explicit action labels to understand how specific inputs generate individual video frames. Researchers at Induction Labs argue that this heavy reliance on labeled action data has created a major bottleneck in scaling interactive AI systems. To overcome this limitation, the company recently introduced "imagination models," an innovative foundation model architecture engineered to pretrain on massive amounts of raw video streams without requiring any accompanying action annotations or supervisory signals.

The primary test system for this architecture is Photon-1, a massive sparse 106-billion-parameter Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) model utilizing 5 billion active parameters per token. Remarkably, from just a single pretraining run on unannotated video, Photon-1 demonstrated an exceptional ability to generalize across highly distinct operational tasks. The system successfully learned to simulate desktop computer environments, execute strategic moves in checkers, and accurately model physical interactions like billiard dynamics, all without prior domain-specific training.

This self-supervised learning milestone highlights a transformative path forward for training generalized world models and interactive control systems. By enabling neural networks to infer underlying physics and system logic directly from visual data, Induction Labs eliminates the need for expensive, labor-intensive dataset labeling. Observers suggest that this approach could dramatically accelerate the deployment of autonomous systems capable of navigating, reasoning, and operating within diverse real-world and digital environments.
