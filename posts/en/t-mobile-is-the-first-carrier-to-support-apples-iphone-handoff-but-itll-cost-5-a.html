T-Mobile Becomes First Carrier to Support Apple’s iPhone Handoff, Adding a $5 Monthly Fee

www.cnet.com · 2026-09-16T09:00:00Z

T-Mobile has introduced network support for Apple's new iPhone Handoff feature for $5 a month, while competitor AT&T currently declines to offer the service.

T-Mobile has become the first wireless carrier to officially introduce network support for Apple’s advanced iPhone Handoff functionality, giving subscribers the ability to transition seamlessly between multiple iPhones. While Apple designed the operating system feature to make managing multiple hardware devices largely frictionless, taking advantage of the service over T-Mobile’s cellular infrastructure will not be free. The telecommunications giant has confirmed that activating the service will add a recurring surcharge of $5 per month to subscriber bills, presenting a new monetization model for ecosystem interoperability.

The feature is geared primarily toward users who carry separate iPhones for personal use and professional duties, allowing calls, cellular data workflows, and messages to move fluidly across devices. By integrating carrier-level synchronization, subscribers avoid the traditional friction of manual forwarding or maintaining completely distinct accounts. However, T-Mobile's decision to monetize this convenience has drawn skepticism from tech observers and customers alike, many of whom expected seamless device switching to be bundled into existing premium data plans.

In contrast, other major American carriers appear to be in no rush to implement the technology. Competitor AT&T has confirmed that it is remaining hands-off for now, having made no public commitments to support the handoff tool or introduce corresponding fee models on its network. As the landscape of mobile device continuity evolves, industry analysts are keenly watching whether rivals will adopt T-Mobile’s fee-based model or potentially use free support as a competitive advantage to lure multi-device power users.
