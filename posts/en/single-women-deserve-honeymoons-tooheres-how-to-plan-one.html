Single Women Deserve Honeymoons Too: How to Plan the Ultimate 'We-Moon'

www.self.com · Fri, 24 Apr 2026 20:08:43 +0000

A growing travel trend encourages single women to celebrate self-love and personal milestones by planning luxurious solo or group 'we-moons' without waiting for traditional marital milestones.

For decades, the honeymoon has been viewed as a rite of passage reserved exclusively for newlyweds embarking on a new chapter of married life. However, a growing cultural shift is redefining this traditional narrative, encouraging single women to claim luxury travel for themselves. Dubbed the "me-moon" or "we-moon," this burgeoning trend celebrates individual milestones, personal growth, and self-love. Women are increasingly embracing the idea that waiting for a romantic partner is unnecessary when it comes to experiencing dream destinations, high-end resorts, and pampering escapes.

Planning a successful single honeymoon requires a strategic combination of self-reflection and practical preparation. Travelers are advised to prioritize personal passions over standard tourist itineraries, selecting destinations that provide a harmonious blend of safety, relaxation, and rich cultural immersion. Whether opting for a secluded wellness retreat, an adventurous solo trek, or an elevated getaway with close female friends, the primary objective remains the same: designing an itinerary that caters entirely to personal joy, rest, and self-empowerment without any compromise.

Beyond the individual benefits, the rise of the single honeymoon reflects a broader movement toward dismantling societal expectations around relationships and travel. The tourism industry is taking notice, with hotels, luxury agencies, and tour operators increasingly offering packages specifically curated for solo female travelers and women's group retreats. Ultimately, taking a "we-moon" serves as a resonant reminder that personal achievements, independence, and self-care are fully deserving of grand celebration, regardless of one's marital status.
