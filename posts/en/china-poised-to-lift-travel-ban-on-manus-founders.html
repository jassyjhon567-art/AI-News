China Set to Lift Travel Ban on Founders of AI Startup Manus

www.ft.com · Sat, 15 Aug 2026 04:00:32 GMT

China is preparing to lift travel restrictions on Manus founders, enabling CEO Xiao Hong to return to Singapore as the AI firm nears resolution of its separation from Meta.

Chinese authorities are reportedly preparing to lift travel restrictions on the founders of artificial intelligence startup Manus, paving the way for Chief Executive Xiao Hong to return to Singapore. The move comes after a period of regulatory scrutiny that had temporarily restricted the movement of the firm's key leadership. Xiao's planned departure marks a significant step forward in restoring standard operational freedom for the executive team.

The easing of travel curbs coincides with Manus making substantial progress toward finalizing its structural separation from Meta. The disengagement process has been a critical focus for the company, as establishing clear organizational independence was seen as vital to satisfying regulatory requirements and stabilizing its cross-border standing. Reaching a resolution on this corporate division appears to have alleviated official concerns, facilitating the lifting of executive travel controls.

With the anticipated removal of the travel ban, Manus is positioned to refocus on its international expansion and regional operations centered in Singapore. The restoration of cross-border mobility for Xiao and the founding team will allow the AI firm to better engage with global partners, secure regional talent, and advance its technology development. The development highlights the complex geopolitical and corporate landscapes that emerging tech enterprises must navigate in the region.
