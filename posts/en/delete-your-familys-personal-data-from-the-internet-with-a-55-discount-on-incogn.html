Incogni offers an automated solution to remove personal data from data broker databases, currently available at a 55 percent discount for subscribers seeking digital privacy.

In the modern digital landscape, personal data has become an increasingly lucrative commodity. Across many jurisdictions, including the United States, commercial entities can legally aggregate, buy, and sell consumer information without requiring direct consent. Data brokers continuously compile extensive profiles containing sensitive details, such as residential addresses, phone numbers, family connections, and financial backgrounds, leaving individuals vulnerable to unwanted marketing and privacy risks.

While consumers theoretically possess the right to demand that companies remove their records from public databases, manually opting out is an arduous and time-consuming endeavor. Each data broker maintains a unique, bureaucratic opt-out procedure, and removed information frequently resurfaces after subsequent data sweeps. For families attempting to safeguard multiple members, tracking and managing dozens of individual removal requests across hundreds of platforms is virtually impossible.

Incogni addresses this systemic issue by automating the entire data deletion process on behalf of its subscribers. The privacy service repeatedly sends legally backed removal requests to data brokers, handles follow-up communications, and monitors databases to ensure deleted information does not reappear.
