Black Friday may have come early this year, but the best travel deals are still to come, according to data from Skyscanner —which found the best day to find discounts on travel isn’t Black Friday or Travel Tuesday. While Travel Tuesday generates plenty of buzz, Skyscanner analyzed billions of proprietary flight data points and found Cyber Monday was the cheapest day to book flights. That day had “the lowest average price per seat” across the five-day Black Friday-to-Travel Tuesday sales period last year, according to Skyscanner, a global travel platform. “Cyber Monday offered the lowest average fares, making it a day worth checking this year,” Lourdes Losada, Skyscanner’s director of the Americas, tells Fast Company . “Start comparing fares before the sales begin so you have a baseline, then monitor prices throughout the period. Knowing what your flight typically costs makes it easier to recognize a worthwhile offer without feeling pressured by a sale’s countdown.” However, Travel Tuesday still had plenty of standout destination-specific savings, with average flights to Fort Lauderdale, Florida; Seoul; and Honolulu dropping 28%, 24%, and 15% respectively, compared with the previous 12-month average. This is an indication that there are strong deals across the entire period. The destination that saw the largest surge in interest was Cabo San Lucas, the Mexican resort town in Baja California Sur. Searches for the location increased 444% across the full Black Friday-to-Travel Tuesday window, compared with the previous 12-month average. Other destinations that Americans searched for on Travel Tuesday included Florence, Italy (interest up 163%); Edinburgh, Scotland (up 162%); Liberia (up 158%), Hanoi, Vietnam (up 147%); and Tokyo (up 132%). Where did American travelers find the biggest Black Friday and Travel Tuesday deals? For travelers looking for savings by destination, the biggest average flight price drops were for Casablanca, Morocco (-30%); Lisbon, Portugal (-28%); Sydney (-21%); Seoul (-17%); and Honolulu (-16%). Skyscanner’s advice for shopping the deals Skyscanner recommends travelers be flexible with dates, set a price alert, stay open to new destinations, and check for alternative departure or arrival airports near your home and destination. The key takeaway is that travelers shouldn’t feel pressure to book on one day, since the entire five-day window provides opportunities that might otherwise be missed, according to Skyscanner. “Black Friday can bring a lot of tempting travel offers and it can also be an overwhelming time to book,” says Laura Lindsay, travel trends and destination expert at Skyscanner. “The key is not to rush, stay open minded and focus on the trip that works best for your budget and plans.”
