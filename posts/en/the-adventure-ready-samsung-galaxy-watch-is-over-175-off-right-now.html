Samsung Galaxy Watch Ultra LTE Drops by Over $175 Following New Model Launch

lifehacker.com · Fri, 16 Oct 2026 19:00:00 +0000

The rugged Samsung Galaxy Watch Ultra LTE has seen its price drop by more than $175, offering premium outdoor features at a significant discount following its successor's release.

Samsung’s rugged flagship wearable, the Galaxy Watch Ultra LTE, is currently available at a substantial discount, falling by more than $175 from its original retail price. The markdown comes as retailers and online storefronts continue to adjust pricing following the debut of the second-generation model. This steady decline provides an attractive opportunity for consumers seeking a durable, high-performance smartwatch without paying standard premium launch rates. For fitness enthusiasts and everyday tech users alike, this steep discount significantly lowers the entry barrier to one of the brand's most resilient devices.

Engineered specifically to endure demanding outdoor conditions, the Galaxy Watch Ultra was introduced to cater to hikers, endurance athletes, and active lifestyle users. The wearable boasts a rugged chassis built with resilient materials, elevated water and dust resistance, and an enhanced display that ensures optimal readability under harsh, direct sunlight. Furthermore, integrated LTE connectivity enables users to send messages, manage phone calls, track routes via GPS, and stream media entirely untethered from their smartphones, solidifying its reputation as a versatile companion for extended outdoor expeditions and rigorous training regimens.

Historically, the transition between hardware cycles offers some of the best value propositions in consumer electronics. While the arrival of a successor often steals the spotlight, the original Ultra still carries advanced biometric sensors, comprehensive health tracking, multi-day battery endurance, and ongoing software support. These core strengths ensure the device remains thoroughly competitive against modern wearable offerings. For buyers seeking high-end performance, robust durability, and reliable connected features at a fraction of the original cost, this current discount makes the smartwatch an exceptionally practical purchase.
