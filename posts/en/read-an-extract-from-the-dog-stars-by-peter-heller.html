Exploring 'The Dog Stars': New Scientist Book Club Features Peter Heller's Post-Apocalyptic Novel

www.newscientist.com · Fri, 31 Jul 2026 08:25:00 +0000

Peter Heller’s post-apocalyptic novel 'The Dog Stars' has been selected as the New Scientist Book Club's August read, featuring pilot Hig navigating a quiet, post-pandemic world.

The New Scientist Book Club has officially chosen Peter Heller’s acclaimed post-apocalyptic novel, The Dog Stars, as its featured selection for the month of August. Set in a hauntingly quiet world following a devastating superflu pandemic that wiped out the vast majority of the global human population, the narrative offers a poignant exploration of isolation, grief, and survival. An exclusive extract released by the publication introduces readers to the atmospheric world crafted by Heller, inviting literature enthusiasts and science fiction fans alike to delve into a deeply moving story of resilience.

At the center of the narrative is Hig, a civilian pilot who navigates the harrowing solitude of a transformed American landscape. Living in an abandoned hangar at a small airport alongside his loyal dog and a stern, heavily armed neighbor, Hig passes his days flying his 1956 Cessna to survey the silent surrounding territory. Despite the devastating loss of his family and the constant operational threats of a degraded society, Hig remains remarkably attuned to nature, driven by a quiet longing for human connection and lingering hope for a better future.

Heller’s celebrated prose masterfully balances the stark realities of post-apocalyptic survival with moments of poetic lyricism and quiet beauty. By selecting The Dog Stars for its monthly read, the New Scientist Book Club aims to spark engaging discussions surrounding environmental disruption, societal vulnerability, and the enduring strength of the human spirit. Readers are encouraged to dive into the published extract to experience Hig's introspective journey firsthand and contribute their perspectives to the book club's growing literary community.
