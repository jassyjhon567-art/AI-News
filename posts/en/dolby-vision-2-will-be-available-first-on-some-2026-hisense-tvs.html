Dolby Vision 2 to Debut First on Select 2026 Hisense Smart TVs

www.theverge.com · 2026-08-06T09:00:00-04:00

Dolby Vision 2 will make its commercial debut on 2026 Hisense TVs before expanding to TCL and Philips models later this year, bringing upgraded HDR performance to consumers.

Dolby Vision 2 is officially preparing for its initial commercial rollout across premium consumer displays, beginning with select 2026 television lineups from Hisense. Originally announced last September, the next-generation dynamic High Dynamic Range (HDR) format aims to elevate visual performance standards for home entertainment systems. Chinese electronics manufacturer Hisense will lead the market adoption by integrating the technology into its upcoming flagship TV series, including the high-end UX, UR9, UR8, and U7 models scheduled for release in 2026.

The updated Dolby Vision 2 standard represents a significant technological leap over the original format, introducing a far more capable image engine. This advanced visual system is designed to deliver superior color precision, refined peak brightness management, and enhanced contrast levels across various ambient lighting conditions. By dynamically optimizing frame-by-frame metadata with greater efficiency, the platform enables filmmakers and content creators to present their work with unprecedented nuance, clarity, and lifelike shadow detail.

Following Hisense's initial debut, other major television brands are quickly preparing to join the ecosystem. Industry giants TCL and Philips have confirmed that they will begin adding Dolby Vision 2 support to select television models later this year. As hardware adoption broadens across manufacturers, the display market is expected to see an increase in optimized streaming titles and gaming content tailored to take full advantage of Dolby's newest HDR standards.
