Dell XPS 13 Review: A Sleek MacBook Competitor That Demands Extra Memory

www.engadget.com · Mon, 10 Aug 2026 12:30:00 +0000

While the Dell XPS 13 offers premium design and impressive portability, upgrading to 16GB of RAM is essential for optimal multitasking and long-term performance.

The latest Dell XPS 13 continues the iconic lineup's long-standing legacy of delivering ultra-premium aesthetics, high-end display quality, and robust portable performance within a strikingly compact form factor. Positioned as a direct Windows rival to Apple's lightweight notebook range, the new XPS 13 stands out immediately with its sleek minimalist industrial design, vibrant edge-to-edge display options, and exceptionally light footprint. It remains a top-tier choice for mobile professionals, digital creators, and students who require a highly portable workstation that makes zero compromises in visual elegance, structural rigidity, or overall hardware craftsmanship.

However, despite its impressive physical design and responsive everyday software performance, the entry-level configuration presents a noticeable limitation for modern computing demands. Equipped with basic standard memory, the base model can experience performance bottlenecks when managing heavy multitasking, dozens of open browser tabs, or resource-heavy productivity applications. To avoid performance degradation and ensure smooth long-term operation, prospective buyers are strongly encouraged to shell out extra money for a 16GB RAM upgrade, which comfortably handles contemporary multi-app workloads and demanding software without system lag.

Ultimately, the Dell XPS 13 solidifies its position as a premier ultraportable laptop that excels in industrial craftsmanship, display brilliance, and sleek portable convenience for everyday users. While upgrading the memory increases the overall purchasing cost, the extra financial investment pays off significantly through fluid daily performance, enhanced system responsiveness, and extended operational longevity over the coming years. For users seeking a stylish, high-performing Windows alternative to Apple's thin-and-light notebooks, a properly configured XPS 13 remains an exceptional and highly rewarding laptop.
