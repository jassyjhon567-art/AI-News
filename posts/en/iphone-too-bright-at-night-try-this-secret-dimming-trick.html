How to Make Your iPhone Screen Even Dimmer Using a Hidden iOS Trick

www.cnet.com · 2019-05-23T13:00:01Z

Learn how to use hidden iOS accessibility features like Zoom filters and Reduce White Point to dim your iPhone screen beyond the standard brightness limit.

Using a smartphone in a pitch-black room often leads to severe eye strain, even when the Control Center screen brightness slider is turned down to its absolute lowest setting. For many iPhone users, the display light remains uncomfortably bright during late-night reading, messaging, or browsing in bed. Fortunately, Apple’s iOS operating system includes a powerful hidden accessibility feature specifically designed to reduce display intensity well beyond the standard minimum brightness limits set by default.

The secret lies within the "Zoom" accessibility settings hidden inside the iOS configuration menu. By enabling the Zoom feature, setting the full-screen region, and adjusting the zoom filter to "Low Light," users can instantly lower the display brightness to a fraction of its standard minimum output. For maximum convenience, this setting can be assigned to the "Accessibility Shortcut," allowing users to simply triple-click the side or home button to toggle the extra-dim mode on and off seamlessly without navigating through settings every night.

Additionally, iOS offers another practical feature known as "Reduce White Point," which effectively decreases the intensity of bright colors and harsh highlights on the screen. Utilizing these built-in accessibility tools not only makes nighttime smartphone usage significantly easier on the eyes but can also help reduce sleep disruption caused by exposure to artificial blue light. By configuring these simple adjustments, iPhone owners can easily customize their device for a far more comfortable, dark-room viewing experience.
