Critical PHP Vulnerabilities Discovered by AI Disrupt Major Websites Worldwide

www.theverge.com · 2026-10-21T16:36:56-04:00

Critical vulnerabilities discovered by AI in the PHP programming language caused widespread disruptions across major internet platforms, including Wikipedia, WordPress-based sites, and numerous smaller blogs.

Two critical software vulnerabilities discovered through artificial intelligence tools have triggered widespread disruptions across the global internet today. The severe security flaws were identified in PHP, an essential server-side programming language that underpins a vast portion of modern digital infrastructure, including any website built on WordPress. The sudden fallout affected an extraordinarily diverse range of online destinations, spanning massive international hubs such as Wikipedia, DeviantArt, and 4chan, down to hyper-local neighborhood blogs and small independent community websites.

Because PHP serves as an indispensable foundational technology for web hosting and software deployment, defects within its core framework can immediately escalate into operational crises for millions of administrators worldwide. The newly discovered bugs were classified as critical vulnerabilities, presenting immediate risks of system instability, service outages, and potential cyber exploitation. As the disruptions spread, webmasters and service providers scrambled to deploy mitigation measures and maintain uptime while software engineering teams mobilized to investigate the root causes and issue emergency patches.

The widespread outage highlights both the structural fragility of internet infrastructure and the transformative role of artificial intelligence in cybersecurity. Although automated intelligence systems were responsible for discovering these profound weaknesses, the subsequent emergency illustrates how deeply interdependent global online networks remain on legacy open-source code. As technical teams continue working to stabilize compromised environments and ensure platform security, the incident serves as an urgent reminder of the necessity for regular maintenance and rapid patch deployment across ubiquitous web technologies.
