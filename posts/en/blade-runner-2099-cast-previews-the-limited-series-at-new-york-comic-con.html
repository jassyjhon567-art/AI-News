'Blade Runner 2099' Stars Michelle Yeoh and Hunter Schafer Unveil First Look at New York Comic Con

www.forbes.com · Sat, 10 Oct 2026 20:47:56 -0400

At New York Comic Con, stars Michelle Yeoh and Hunter Schafer unveiled the trailer and shared insights for the highly anticipated limited series 'Blade Runner 2099.'

The cast and creative team behind the upcoming television event "Blade Runner 2099" took center stage at New York Comic Con on Saturday to offer eager fans an exclusive preview of the limited series. Headlined by acclaimed stars Michelle Yeoh and Hunter Schafer, the high-profile panel marked one of the convention's most anticipated appearances, formally introducing the next chapter in the groundbreaking cyberpunk franchise. Both actors engaged with the packed auditorium, reflecting on the enduring cultural impact of the franchise and the immense responsibility of bringing its atmospheric world to a new generation of viewers.

During the panel discussion, the audience was treated to the world premiere of the series' first official trailer, showcasing a visually striking vision of the dystopian future filled with neon-soaked cityscapes and moody tension. Both Yeoh and Schafer shared insights into their respective roles and discussed the collaborative process of translating the cinematic mythos into an episodic format. They reflected on the narrative depth of the project, noting how the new storyline honors the philosophical inquiries regarding identity, memory, and artificial life that define the saga.

Serving as a direct television continuation of Ridley Scott's iconic 1982 film and Denis Villeneuve's acclaimed 2017 follow-up, "Blade Runner 2049," this new limited series represents an ambitious evolution for the storied science-fiction property. The presentation drew overwhelmingly positive reactions from convention attendees, generating substantial momentum across industry observers and passionate fans alike. With key details now coming into clearer focus, anticipation continues to build ahead of the show's eventual streaming premiere.
