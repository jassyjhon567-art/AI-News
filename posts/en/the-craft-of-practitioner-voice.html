The Craft of Practitioner Voice: Jim Highsmith Expands on Technical Writing and Storytelling

martinfowler.com · 2026-10-13T10:06:00-04:00

Jim Highsmith has expanded his guide on practitioner writing, outlining core principles, storytelling techniques, sensible AI usage, and three editing levels to help professionals share their expertise effectively.

Following his initial exploration of how professionals communicate with their peers, software pioneer and author Jim Highsmith has expanded his thesis on practitioner-oriented literature. Months after first introducing the concept that practitioners writing for one another represent a distinct literary style, Highsmith has added a comprehensive section detailing the specific craft required to master this approach. The updated work focuses deeply on how an author's authentic persona emerges across the page, helping professionals articulate their specialized, field-tested knowledge with greater clarity and authority.

At the heart of Highsmith’s updated guide are four foundational principles designed to preserve genuine practitioner presence. He highlights the transformative role of storytelling, explaining that real-world narratives ground abstract frameworks in tangible reality. Furthermore, Highsmith addresses contemporary writing practices by offering practical guidelines on how to sensibly incorporate artificial intelligence into the drafting process. Rather than relying blindly on automated tools, authors are encouraged to maintain creative control, ensuring that their relationship with their target audience remains direct, credible, and appropriately tailored.

To refine raw drafts into polished, high-impact literature, the guide introduces a structured framework featuring three distinct levels of editing. These stages prompt authors to evaluate their manuscripts sequentially, addressing broad thematic coherence, structural transitions, and fine-grained line editing. By bridging the gap between hands-on field experience and methodical communication, Highsmith provides a valuable roadmap for industry specialists who wish to articulate their hard-won wisdom, fostering richer professional dialogue and shared learning across technical communities.
