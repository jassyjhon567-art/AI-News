Six Ready-Made Meals Recalled at Publix, Kroger, and Food Lion Over Foreign Material Contamination

www.inc.com · Wed, 05 Aug 2026 16:59:33 -0400

Six ready-made meal products sold at Publix, Kroger, and Food Lion have been recalled across multiple states following the discovery of metal aluminum slivers by inspectors.

Food safety authorities have issued an urgent recall for six varieties of pre-packaged ready-made meals distributed to major supermarket chains across multiple states, including Publix, Kroger, and Food Lion. The voluntary recall was initiated after inspectors discovered small aluminum slivers inside select packaged food items during routine quality and safety assessments. Consumers who recently purchased these ready-to-eat meal options from these popular retail outlets are being strongly advised to check their refrigerators and freezers carefully to avoid consuming potentially hazardous items.

The presence of foreign metallic materials such as aluminum slivers poses a significant health hazard to consumers, potentially causing dental injuries, lacerations in the mouth or throat, and internal gastrointestinal complications if accidentally ingested. According to regulatory statements, the nationwide recall affects specific batch numbers and expiration dates distributed across several retail locations. Store operators have already begun removing the impacted products from store shelves, while comprehensive inventory audits are currently ongoing to ensure that no affected units remain available for purchase.

Health officials and retail management urge customers who have purchased these specific ready-made meals to either discard them immediately or return them to the place of purchase for a full refund. Although no severe injuries or adverse health events related to the contamination have been officially reported to date, strict precautions are being enforced to safeguard public health. Consumers seeking additional details regarding lot numbers, product identification, or refund policies are encouraged to contact the retailer customer service teams or consult official safety notices.
