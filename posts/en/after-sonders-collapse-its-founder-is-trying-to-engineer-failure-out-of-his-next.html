After Sonder's Downfall, Founder Francis Davidson Launches Odessia to Avoid Past Pitfalls

www.inc.com · Mon, 03 Aug 2026 05:00:00 -0400

Following the collapse of his $600 million hospitality company Sonder, founder Francis Davidson is launching a new startup, Odessia, designed to prevent past operational mistakes.

Francis Davidson, the visionary entrepreneur who previously built the short-term rental and hospitality company Sonder into a $600 million business, is embarking on a fresh venture following the company's dramatic downfall. With his new startup, named Odessia, Davidson is determined to apply the tough lessons learned from his past experiences. Rather than stepping back from entrepreneurship, he is leveraging his previous setbacks to construct a brand-new business model engineered specifically to withstand failure and avoid the fatal mistakes that previously overwhelmed his original business.

Sonder’s rapid expansion was initially celebrated, but the company eventually encountered severe operational challenges, burdensome master leases, and shifting macroeconomic conditions. These structural pressures created mounting financial strain that the business ultimately could not sustain. In founding Odessia, Davidson is introducing strategic mechanisms meant to eliminate these exact vulnerabilities. The new venture emphasizes financial flexibility, optimized capital deployment, and reduced exposure to high fixed costs, ensuring that the company remains resilient even during broader economic downturns.

The emergence of Odessia reflects a mature shift in the broader startup ecosystem, where second-time founders are increasingly prioritizing long-term stability and sustainable unit economics over reckless scale. By openly confronting the structural flaws that led to Sonder’s decline, Davidson hopes to rebuild trust among prospective investors and industry partners. While building a resilient business in a volatile market presents inherent difficulties, Odessia represents an ambitious attempt to transform a high-profile corporate failure into a durable, future-proof blueprint for startup success.
