LEGO Retires Popular 'Hocus Pocus' Cottage Set: Where Collectors Can Still Find It

www.goodhousekeeping.com · Tue, 13 Oct 2026 21:43:10 +0000

Although LEGO has officially retired its popular Sanderson Sisters' cottage set from 'Hocus Pocus,' collectors can still track down remaining copies through secondary marketplaces and specialty retailers.

LEGO has officially retired its beloved Disney "Hocus Pocus: The Sanderson Sisters' Cottage" set, bringing an end to its active production run. Inspired by the iconic 1993 fantasy comedy film, the elaborate construction kit quickly became a major favorite among pop culture enthusiasts and dedicated brick builders upon its initial launch. However, as is customary with many licensed and thematic collectibles, the company has cleared the set from its active catalog to make space for fresh offerings, sending devoted collectors into a race to secure remaining inventory.

The build earned significant praise for its meticulous recreation of the film's eerie cottage, complete with distinct architectural details, atmospheric interior rooms, and authentic character minifigures representing the infamous witch trio and their companions. With the set officially out of production, primary retail stores and the official manufacturer website have exhausted their supplies. This transition has naturally pushed the model into collector status, where its scarcity on conventional store shelves has begun driving interest across secondary resale platforms.

For those still eager to add the witchy display piece to their shelves, alternative retail avenues offer viable solutions. Trusted secondary marketplaces, specialized collector storefronts, and independent online retailers frequently have factory-sealed boxes available. However, prospective buyers should act with care by reviewing seller ratings, confirming packaging authenticity, and comparing market listings, as retired pop culture merchandise frequently commands a noticeable premium over its original recommended retail price. Navigating these channels patiently can still reward persistent fans with an authentic piece of movie nostalgia.
