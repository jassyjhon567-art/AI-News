How an Army Veteran Turned a Creative Coping Mechanism into a $175,000 Soap Business

www.businessinsider.com · 2026-11-20T22:45:01Z

Army veteran Cai Valdez transformed her therapeutic soap-making hobby into a thriving enterprise, putting her on track to earn $175,000 just eighteen months after launching the business.

For many military service members, transitioning back to civilian life brings profound emotional and psychological challenges. Cai Valdez, a former educator and United States Army veteran, experienced significant post-traumatic stress disorder following the conclusion of her military service. Searching for an effective, grounded outlet to process trauma and manage daily anxiety, she turned to the methodical craft of handmade soap-making. The repetitive and sensory nature of formulating soaps provided a calming focus, helping her navigate difficult days and discover unexpected therapeutic relief.

What began strictly as a personal coping mechanism soon evolved into an ambitious creative endeavor. Valdez began experimenting with artisanal recipes at home, prioritizing skin-friendly ingredients, distinctive scents, and careful craftsmanship. As interest in her products expanded through word of mouth, she identified a viable commercial opportunity. Drawing on the organizational discipline and resilience developed during her time in uniform and the classroom, she transitioned the hobby into a dedicated side hustle. She leveraged digital platforms to showcase her production process, steadily building a loyal customer base.

Just eighteen months after formally establishing the venture, Valdez is now on track to generate roughly $175,000 in revenue. The business has exceeded initial expectations, moving from a modest home setup into a flourishing enterprise with a dedicated following. Beyond its commercial success, her story offers an inspiring blueprint for veterans and individuals coping with mental health difficulties, demonstrating how a pursuit rooted in healing can transform into a sustainable, thriving professional journey.
