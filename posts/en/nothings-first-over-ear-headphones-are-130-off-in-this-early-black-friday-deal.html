Early Black Friday Deal Drops Nothing’s Debut Over-Ear Headphones to Record-Low $169

lifehacker.com · Fri, 20 Nov 2026 14:00:00 +0000

Nothing’s first over-ear headphones have dropped to a record-low price of $169 ahead of Black Friday, featuring hybrid noise cancellation, custom EQ, and a $130 discount.

Tech brand Nothing has introduced a massive price cut on its very first pair of over-ear headphones, bringing the premium audio accessory to a record-low price of $169 as part of early Black Friday promotions. The substantial $130 discount slashes the original price tag significantly, offering prospective buyers an enticing entry point into the company's expanding audio portfolio. With the busy holiday shopping season quickly approaching, this limited-time markdown delivers high-end personal audio capabilities at a much more accessible price point for everyday listeners and tech enthusiasts alike.

Known for its minimalist and distinctive aesthetic, Nothing's debut over-ear model combines a standout visual design with a host of capable performance features. The headphones boast hybrid active noise cancellation technology, effectively reducing ambient background noise during daily commutes, office work, or travel. In addition to sound isolation, the device offers deep equalizer customization options, enabling users to finely calibrate frequency ranges, enhance bass response, and tailor the overall audio profile to suit diverse musical tastes and listening habits.

The aggressive discounting strategy places Nothing's over-ear headphones in direct competition with established industry stalwarts in the mid-range to premium wireless audio market. By combining premium build quality, modern styling, and robust sound customizability at a sub-$200 threshold, the headphones present exceptional value for money. As holiday discounts continue to ramp up across major retail platforms, this deal stands out as one of the most notable audio bargains for consumers eager to upgrade their personal listening gear without breaking the bank.
