Apple Overtakes Nvidia as World's Most Valuable Company, Approaching $5 Trillion Market Cap

9to5mac.com · Mon, 27 Jul 2026 14:14:43 +0000

Apple has reclaimed its status as the world's most valuable company, surpassing Nvidia in market capitalization as its valuation approaches the $5 trillion threshold.

Apple has officially reclaimed its title as the world's most valuable publicly traded company by market capitalization, overtaking semiconductor giant Nvidia during intraday trading on Friday. The surge in Apple's valuation was propelled by a strong weekly performance, with the company's stock rising by 17 points. This notable shift marks yet another high-stakes exchange at the top of Wall Street's leaderboard, coming just ten days after Nvidia had temporarily surpassed Apple to claim the supreme ranking among global corporate giants.

The recent stock rally has positioned Apple on the verge of a historic financial milestone as it barrels toward a $5 trillion market capitalization. Although the tech giant has not officially crossed the $5 trillion threshold yet, intraday trading pushed its total valuation to approximately $4.944 trillion. Investors have rallied behind the company, driven by sustained confidence in its broader device ecosystem, services revenue growth, and strategic position within the rapidly evolving consumer technology landscape.

The rapid back-and-forth swapping of top market positions between Apple and Nvidia underscores the dynamic nature of current equity markets and the vast scale of modern tech conglomerates. While Nvidia's explosive growth has been largely driven by the unprecedented demand for artificial intelligence chips, Apple's resilient cash flow and consumer reach continue to solidify its dominance. As both industry leaders navigate shifting macroeconomic conditions, their ongoing race for market supremacy remains a primary focus for international investors and market analysts.
