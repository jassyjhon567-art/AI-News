Ninja Unveils Practical Magic-Inspired Slushie Machine Following Rapid Sellout of Original Model

www.cnet.com · 2026-08-21T12:00:00Z

Ninja has launched a Practical Magic-themed slushie machine in new shades ahead of the upcoming sequel, following the rapid two-hour sellout of its original model.

Ninja has officially launched a special edition of its popular frozen beverage machine, drawing creative inspiration from the cult classic film Practical Magic. The brand decided to unveil this new collection after the original model experienced unprecedented consumer interest, completely selling out within just two hours of its initial market debut. This uniquely styled kitchen appliance seeks to capture the renewed excitement and nostalgia surrounding the beloved supernatural franchise ahead of its highly anticipated sequel.

Created to commemorate the enduring legacy of the film, the new slushie maker is being introduced in several spellbinding shades and aesthetic finishes that pay homage to the movie's magical world. Scheduled strategically ahead of the Practical Magic 2 release this September, the device seamlessly combines everyday frozen drink preparation with pop-culture charm. It is engineered to allow users to craft a wide variety of customized frozen treats, slushes, and themed cocktails directly on their kitchen countertops.

The overwhelming response and rapid sellout of the initial model highlighted an increasing demand for crossover products that bridge home culinary gadgets and entertainment fandoms. By offering these new colorways, Ninja aims to satisfy persistent consumer demand while providing movie enthusiasts and beverage lovers with an eye-catching appliance. Retail industry watchers expect this limited-edition release to generate strong consumer interest as the franchise prepares for its upcoming return.
