Building a Startup from Scratch: The Story Behind Askors and the Future of Reservations

dev.to · Sun, 02 Aug 2026 02:24:54 +0000

Developer Aeiou has launched Askors, a startup aimed at streamlining business reservations into a simple messaging experience, while committing to document the entrepreneurial journey publicly for fellow creators.

Launching a new tech venture often stems from a desire to solve everyday friction, a realization that inspired developer Aeiou to transition from side projects to founding a startup called Askors. The platform was born out of a simple observation regarding how fragmented and cumbersome reservation systems remain for modern consumers. Currently, booking a service or securing a spot at a venue depends heavily on individual business operations, requiring phone calls, social media direct messages, or tedious online forms with delayed confirmations. Askors addresses this inefficiency by aiming to make the entire reservation process as effortless as sending a single message.

Building Askors from the ground up has reshaped the founder's perspective on software development, proving that writing clean code is merely one component of creating a viable product. The entrepreneurial journey has necessitated a deep dive into product design, user experience optimization, and direct business outreach. Engaging directly with prospective clients has brought valuable insights alongside constructive feedback and inevitable rejection. Navigating these challenges has highlighted the importance of listening to market demands and continuously refining the platform to build something that truly serves user needs.

Moving forward, Aeiou plans to transparently document the entire building process on the DEV Community platform. This ongoing series will cover technical architecture, product strategy decisions, operational mistakes, and pivotal milestones. By sharing both victories and setbacks openly, the founder hopes to provide a practical reference point and source of encouragement for other developers embarking on their own startup journeys.
