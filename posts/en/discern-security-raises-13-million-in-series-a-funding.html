Discern Security Secures $13 Million in Series A Funding to Advance Its Agentic Cybersecurity Platform

www.securityweek.com · Thu, 30 Jul 2026 13:00:00 +0000

Cybersecurity firm Discern Security has raised $13 million in Series A funding to accelerate the development and market adoption of its autonomous agentic security platform.

Cybersecurity enterprise Discern Security has announced the successful completion of a $13 million Series A funding round, marking a significant milestone in its growth trajectory. The capital raise underscores the rising demand for innovative security management solutions capable of handling the intricacies of modern digital infrastructures. This strategic investment will enable the organization to scale its core technology offerings, broaden its market footprint, and better support enterprises navigating increasingly complex security operational environments.

The primary focus of this funding will be to accelerate the research, development, and commercial adoption of Discern Security’s cutting-edge agentic platform. Engineered to transform traditional cybersecurity management, the platform utilizes autonomous agents to streamline policy management, continuously analyze security posture, and optimize configurations across disparate security tools. By automating these critical functions, Discern Security aims to eliminate operational siloes, mitigate systemic vulnerabilities, and significantly lighten the operational workload burdened by modern security operations teams.

As organizations continue to adopt diverse multi-vendor security stacks, the need for centralized intelligence and automated policy enforcement has become paramount. Discern Security plans to leverage the newly acquired funds to expand its global workforce, hiring specialized talent across engineering, product development, and go-to-market teams. Furthermore, the company intends to forge deeper strategic industry partnerships to ensure widespread integration and deliver long-term value to its growing enterprise customer base.
