Disney Unveils Major Theme Park Expansions: New Lands and Attractions Coming in 2026 and Beyond

www.cnet.com · 2025-12-31T13:05:45Z

Disney has announced an ambitious lineup of new lands and attractions arriving in 2026 and beyond, featuring Monstropolis, Villains Land, and expanded Marvel experiences.

Disney has revealed an extensive slate of major theme park expansions scheduled to roll out across Disneyland and Walt Disney World starting in 2026 and extending well into the decade. This ambitious development wave represents one of the largest capital investment initiatives in the history of Disney Experiences, aiming to integrate beloved franchises from Pixar, Marvel, and classic Disney storytelling deeper into the guest experience.

Among the most prominent additions is the creation of Monstropolis, bringing the vibrant monster universe of "Monsters, Inc." to life with the company's first-ever suspended roller coaster. At Walt Disney World, Magic Kingdom will introduce an entirely dedicated Villains Land spotlighting iconic Disney antagonists, while an overhauled Tropical Americas section will feature an all-new Indiana Jones adventure. Simultaneously, Disney California Adventure is set to expand with new Marvel-themed Avengers attractions and a sprawling Piston Peak area inspired by the "Cars" animated universe.

Theme park leadership emphasized that these upcoming lands and ride systems are engineered to increase overall resort capacity while introducing next-generation technology. Construction is scheduled to progress in phases, with the earliest experiences opening in 2026. The widespread enhancements are expected to transform the guest journey across both domestic resort destinations for years to come.
