Cloud Startup Railway Raises $100M to Challenge AWS in the AI Era

venturebeat.com · Thu, 22 Jan 2026 14:00:00 GMT

Cloud platform Railway raised $100 million in Series B funding to scale its AI-native infrastructure and compete with legacy cloud providers like AWS and Google Cloud.

San Francisco-based cloud platform Railway has secured $100 million in a Series B funding round led by TQ Ventures, marking a major milestone for the startup as artificial intelligence transforms software development. The funding round also saw participation from FPV Ventures, Redpoint, and Unusual Ventures. Designed to address the growing limitations of legacy cloud infrastructure, Railway plans to utilize the fresh capital to expand its physical data center footprint, scale its team, and challenge established tech giants like Amazon Web Services and Google Cloud.

Founded by 28-year-old Chief Executive Jake Cooper, Railway has quietly amassed over two million developers, largely through word-of-mouth growth. The platform sets itself apart by offering sub-second deployment times, overcoming traditional bottlenecks where building and deploying code on legacy platforms can take several minutes. To support this speed, Railway took the uncommon step of entirely departing public cloud providers to build its own proprietary data center infrastructure, enabling vertical integration and per-second billing that significantly reduces operational costs for developers and enterprises.

Operating with a streamlined team of only 30 employees, Railway currently processes more than 10 million deployments each month and generates tens of millions in annual revenue. The startup claims that roughly 31 percent of Fortune 500 companies now use its infrastructure alongside numerous emerging AI ventures. With $100 million in new funding, Railway intends to build out its first dedicated sales and marketing operation, expand its global network reach, and capitalize on the massive surge of software being created by modern AI coding assistants.
