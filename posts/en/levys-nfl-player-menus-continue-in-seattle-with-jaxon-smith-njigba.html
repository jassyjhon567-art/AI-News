Levy Expands NFL Player-Curated Menus in Seattle Featuring Jaxon Smith-Njigba

www.forbes.com · Sat, 10 Oct 2026 11:18:14 -0400

Levy has partnered with Seahawks receiver Jaxon Smith-Njigba to introduce three player-curated dishes at Seattle's Lumen Field, enhancing the fan dining experience.

Hospitality partner Levy has expanded its innovative NFL player menu series at Seattle’s Lumen Field, this time joining forces with prominent Seahawks wide receiver Jaxon Smith-Njigba. The latest partnership introduces three unique, specially crafted dishes to the stadium's game-day concession lineup, presenting football fans with an elevated culinary experience throughout the season. By directly integrating the athlete's personal palate and background into the food offerings, the program seeks to build memorable and authentic connections between local athletes and their passionate supporters.

The newly unveiled menu selections highlight Smith-Njigba’s favorite flavors and beloved comfort foods, offering game attendees a distinctive taste of the dishes that inspire the young receiver off the field. Levy’s experienced on-site culinary team collaborated closely with Smith-Njigba to develop recipes that uphold rigorous venue standards while delivering bold, player-inspired culinary profiles. This creative approach allows visitors at Lumen Field to connect with their favorite players in a completely novel way, successfully transforming traditional concession stops into an engaging and immersive part of the overall stadium atmosphere.

This collaborative venture represents a broader, evolving movement across professional sports venues, where hospitality leaders are continuously working to redefine traditional stadium food. Rather than relying solely on conventional game-day snacks, venues are actively embracing customized, athlete-driven culinary concepts that resonate strongly with regional communities. Levy’s continued execution of the player menu concept in Seattle reinforces its commitment to modern sports hospitality, providing fans at Lumen Field with an enriched dining experience that celebrates team identity on and off the gridiron.
