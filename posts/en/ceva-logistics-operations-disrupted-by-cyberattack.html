Cyberattack Disrupts Ceva Logistics Operations Across Europe

www.securityweek.com · Wed, 12 Aug 2026 11:37:28 +0000

A cyberattack targeting Ceva Logistics' European operations affected eight warehouses, causing system outages and shipment delays for multiple corporate customers across the region.

Global supply chain and freight management enterprise Ceva Logistics has experienced significant operational disruptions following a recent cyberattack that targeted its European contract logistics business. The security incident directly compromised digital operations across eight major warehouse facilities within the region, forcing IT teams to take affected infrastructure offline in an effort to contain the threat and prevent further unauthorized system access.

The sudden technical outage severely impacted automated inventory management, processing systems, and cargo dispatch workflows. As a result, several corporate clients utilizing Ceva's distribution network have faced unexpected shipment delays and logistical backlogs. In response, the company deployed dedicated emergency response teams and external cybersecurity experts to conduct a thorough digital forensics investigation, isolate compromised networks, and systematically restore operational functionality while keeping clients updated on recovery progress.

While Ceva Logistics has not yet disclosed specific details regarding the exact nature of the cyber incident or any potential threat actor involvement, the breach underscores the increasing cyber risks faced by global logistics and transport organizations. Industry experts emphasize that as supply chain networks become heavily dependent on integrated digital software and automated warehouse technology, robust cybersecurity measures are essential to protecting critical infrastructure and maintaining seamless international trade flow.
