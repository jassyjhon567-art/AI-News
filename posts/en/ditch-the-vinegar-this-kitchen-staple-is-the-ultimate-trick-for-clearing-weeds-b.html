Forget Vinegar: This Common Kitchen Staple Is the Ultimate Solution for Paver Weeds

www.tomsguide.com · Mon, 19 Oct 2026 11:33:52 +0000

Simple table salt offers an effective, chemical-free kitchen alternative to vinegar for eliminating stubborn weeds and preventing regrowth between patio pavers.

Weeds emerging through the narrow gaps between patio pavers and walkway stones are a persistent nuisance for homeowners and gardeners alike. While chemical herbicides offer a quick and aggressive fix, their potential harm to household pets, beneficial insects, and the broader environment makes many gardeners hesitant to spray them around living spaces. Even popular natural remedies like white vinegar often fall short of expectations, burning only the top foliage while leaving hardy root systems intact to regrow just weeks later.

Fortunately, a far more durable and effective remedy is likely already sitting in your kitchen pantry: simple table salt. When poured directly into the joints between paving slabs, salt acts as a potent natural desiccant. By drawing essential moisture away from plant cells, it rapidly dehydrates unwanted vegetation down to the very base of the root system. Furthermore, because the salt settles into the crevices, it creates an inhospitable barrier that prevents dormant seeds from germinating, offering lasting protection against future weed growth.

Applying this pantry staple requires minimal effort, but precision is vital to protect nearby garden beds. Homeowners should target only the weed-infested joints on a clear, sunny afternoon when rain is not expected to wash the runoff into surrounding turf or delicate flowers. Sweeping the granules neatly into the cracks and lightly dampening them with water helps the solution sink deep into the soil. With sensible application, this common household item delivers a pristine, weed-free patio without the cost or risks of synthetic sprays.
