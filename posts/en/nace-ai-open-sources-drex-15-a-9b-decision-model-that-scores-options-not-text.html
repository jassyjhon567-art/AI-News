Nace AI Releases Drex 1.5, an Open-Source 9B Decision Model Built to Score Options Directly

www.marktechpost.com · Sat, 10 Oct 2026 04:51:34 +0000

Nace AI has open-sourced Drex 1.5, a 9-billion-parameter decision model that scores multiple options in a single forward pass across a 128K context window.

Artificial intelligence startup Nace AI has officially open-sourced Drex 1.5, an innovative 9-billion-parameter decision model designed to streamline option evaluation. Unlike conventional large language models that primarily focus on sequential text generation, Drex 1.5 is purpose-built to evaluate distinct choices directly. Its architecture allows it to return a calibrated probability score for every provided option within just a single forward pass, substantially reducing computational overhead in structured decision tasks.

In terms of technical benchmarks and operational capacity, Drex 1.5 demonstrates competitive capabilities for specialized reasoning. The model secured an impressive score of 58.08 on Decision Index 0.3.1, underscoring its competence across complex, multi-faceted decision-making benchmarks. Additionally, the system supports an expansive context window of up to 128,000 (128K) tokens, permitting the ingestion of extensive documentation, background data, and multi-turn conversational history before reaching an assessment.

By releasing the weights of Drex 1.5 to the wider community, Nace AI provides researchers and developers with an efficient alternative to standard autoregressive models. Traditional generative approaches often consume disproportionate compute resources when selecting from predefined candidates. In contrast, Drex 1.5 offers a targeted, low-latency framework well-suited for autonomous agents, automated workflows, and complex routing systems that require reliable, direct option scoring without unnecessary generative verbosity.
