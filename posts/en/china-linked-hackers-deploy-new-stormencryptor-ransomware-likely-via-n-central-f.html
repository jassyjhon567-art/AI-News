China-Linked Cybercriminals Deploy New StormEncryptor Ransomware via N-Central Vulnerability

thehackernews.com · Mon, 10 Aug 2026 22:08:37 +0530

Microsoft has warned that China-linked threat group Storm-1175 is deploying a new C++ ransomware strain called StormEncryptor, likely exploiting vulnerabilities in N-central remote management software.

Microsoft Threat Intelligence has uncovered a new ransomware strain named StormEncryptor, which is actively being deployed by a financially motivated cybercriminal group known as Storm-1175. Researchers have linked the threat actor to China and noted that the group is likely gaining initial access to enterprise networks by exploiting critical security vulnerabilities in N-central management software. This newly observed campaign represents a significant operational shift for Storm-1175, which had previously relied on the widely distributed Medusa ransomware strain to carry out its extortion attacks.

Technical analysis reveals that StormEncryptor is written in C++ and specifically engineered to encrypt sensitive files across infected corporate systems. Once executed on a host, the malware systematically locks victim data and appends the ".encrypted" file extension to all targeted files, rendering them completely inaccessible without a specialized decryption key. The development and deployment of this custom, previously undocumented strain indicate that the operators are actively upgrading their toolset to bypass conventional security controls and enhance their operational efficiency.

The emergence of StormEncryptor highlights an ongoing trend of nation-state-linked actors participating in lucrative cybercrime enterprises alongside traditional espionage activities. Microsoft continues to track Storm-1175's infrastructure and strongly advises organizations using remote monitoring and management tools like N-central to apply security updates immediately. Security experts further stress the importance of implementing multi-factor authentication, robust network monitoring, and secure backup solutions to defend against sophisticated ransomware operations.
