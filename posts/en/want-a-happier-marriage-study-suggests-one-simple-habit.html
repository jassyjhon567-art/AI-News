Want a Stronger, Happier Marriage? Exercising Together May Be the Key, Study Finds

phys.org · Tue, 11 Aug 2026 08:40:02 EDT

A new BYU study reveals that exercising together can significantly strengthen marital bonds by fostering emotional connection, teamwork, and mutual encouragement among couples.

It is widely established that engaging in regular physical activity provides extensive, long-lasting benefits for both bodily health and mental well-being. However, recent scientific evidence indicates that the positive impacts of staying active may extend far beyond individual wellness and directly influence the quality of romantic relationships. A compelling new study conducted by researchers at Brigham Young University (BYU) suggests that physical exercise can significantly strengthen marital bonds, especially when spouses make a consistent habit of exercising together.

The study highlights how participating in shared physical activities fosters deeper emotional connection, clearer communication, and higher overall relationship satisfaction among married partners. By approaching fitness routines as a unified team, couples tend to offer each other continuous encouragement and positive accountability, creating shared achievements that enrich their daily interactions. This collaborative physical effort effectively helps mitigate everyday stress, elevates overall mood through natural endorphin release, and cultivates a profound sense of teamwork that reinforces the foundation of an enduring, loving marriage.

While engaging in physical exercise individually still contributes positively to personal health and well-being, the research underscores the unique interpersonal advantages that emerge when couples intentionally align their health habits. Relationship experts suggest that even simple joint routines, such as taking regular neighborhood walks, going on joint bike rides, or attending gym sessions as a pair, can yield meaningful long-term improvements in marital harmony. Ultimately, integrating shared physical fitness into everyday life provides an accessible, enjoyable, and powerful strategy for building a happier, healthier, and more resilient partnership.
