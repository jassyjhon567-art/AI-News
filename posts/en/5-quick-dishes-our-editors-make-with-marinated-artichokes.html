5 Quick and Flavorful Dishes to Make With Marinated Artichokes

www.bonappetit.com · Sun, 18 Oct 2026 10:00:00 +0000

Food editors highlight marinated artichokes as a versatile pantry staple, using them to quickly elevate dishes from tangy potato salads to crisp, savory galettes.

Marinated artichoke hearts are among the most versatile pantry staples, offering a punchy combination of acidity, herbs, and tender texture right out of the jar. Home cooks often relegate them to simple appetizer platters, but culinary editors frequently rely on them as an effortless shortcut to elevate everyday meals. Infused with olive oil and aromatic seasonings, these tender vegetables can instantly impart depth to a wide variety of quick weeknight recipes without the need for extensive prep work.

Among the editors' favorite creations is a tangy, briny twist on the classic potato salad. By tossing warm, boiled potatoes directly with chopped marinated artichokes and a splash of their flavorful packing oil, the spuds absorb a vibrant, zesty finish without needing heavy mayonnaise. Another standout dish is a golden, semi-homemade savory galette. Using store-bought puff pastry or pie dough, cooks can layer the artichokes alongside creamy cheeses like ricotta or goat cheese, baking until crisp and deeply browned.

Beyond baked goods and side salads, marinated artichokes seamlessly transition into warm pasta tosses, blended dips, and blistered flatbreads. Sautéing them briefly with garlic, cherry tomatoes, and red pepper flakes creates an instant sauce for long pasta varieties, while pureeing them with white beans produces a vibrant spread. Because the pre-seasoned oil does double duty for both cooking and dressing, this reliable ingredient continues to prove that sophisticated dining does not require hours in the kitchen.
