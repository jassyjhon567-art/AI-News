Bringing Google Pixel's Iconic 'Now Playing' Feature to the Galaxy S24 Ultra

www.makeuseof.com · Mon, 19 Oct 2026 11:30:17 GMT

Users can now recreate Google Pixel's ambient 'Now Playing' music recognition on the Galaxy S24 Ultra, delivering a near-identical and convenient lock-screen experience.

One of the most beloved and distinctive features of Google’s Pixel lineup is "Now Playing," an ambient capability that automatically identifies songs playing in the surrounding environment directly on the lock screen without requiring any manual user input. For years, this seamless, privacy-focused, and battery-friendly music recognition has remained a coveted exclusive for Pixel owners. However, tech enthusiasts have increasingly sought to bridge the software divide, successfully finding clever methods to bring a remarkably similar ambient listening functionality to rival high-end flagships, most notably Samsung's flagship Galaxy S24 Ultra.

By taking advantage of specialized third-party applications and flexible background automation tools, users can effectively recreate this continuous music detection experience on Samsung’s One UI platform. Rather than needing to unlock the phone and launch a music identification utility whenever an appealing song catches their attention, the setup quietly monitors ambient sound and immediately surfaces artist and track information on the display. This subtle enhancement effectively transforms the Galaxy S24 Ultra’s lock screen into a knowledgeable companion that reliably identifies the surrounding audio landscape in real time.

To be sure, this workaround is not a completely identical duplicate of Google's custom on-device machine learning models, which rely on a local database and dedicated low-power processing architecture. Nevertheless, the alternative implementation proves remarkably swift, accurate, and reasonably gentle on daily battery consumption. For Galaxy S24 Ultra owners who enjoy the deep customizability of the Android ecosystem, capturing this beloved Pixel hallmark provides a deeply satisfying experience, seamlessly combining Google’s intuitive convenience with Samsung’s versatile hardware prowess.
