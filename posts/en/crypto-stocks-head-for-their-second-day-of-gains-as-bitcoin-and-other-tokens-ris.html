Crypto-Related Stocks Surge for Second Day as Bitcoin and Major Tokens Rally

www.fastcompany.com · 2026-08-21T12:22:00

Crypto-related stocks and major digital tokens extended gains for a second day after expanded U.S. Treasury bond buybacks spurred liquidity and pushed investors toward riskier assets.

The cryptocurrency market is witnessing an extended rally, with major tokens like Bitcoin, Ethereum, and XRP posting strong consecutive gains. This upward momentum follows a key policy announcement by the U.S. Treasury to double its bond buyback program from $2 billion to $4 billion per operation for long-term bonds. Designed to inject market liquidity, the expanded program has pushed bond yields lower, encouraging investors seeking higher returns to move capital out of fixed income and into higher-risk digital assets.

The surge in digital asset valuations has provided a significant boost to crypto-adjacent publicly traded equities. Major trading platforms, including Coinbase and Robinhood, saw their stock prices climb as heightened retail and institutional trading activity points to higher transaction fee revenues. Similarly, corporate treasury holders and infrastructure providers like Circle, Strategy Inc., and Bitmine Immersion Technologies posted notable advances, while prominent mining firms such as MARA Holdings, Riot Platforms, and CleanSpark also extended sharp gains.

Despite the renewed enthusiasm across digital asset markets, industry observers remain cautious regarding the longevity of the rebound. Cryptocurrencies and related equities are characterized by significant volatility, leaving them vulnerable to sudden pullbacks. Furthermore, broader macroeconomic headwinds—including lingering inflation concerns, geopolitical uncertainty, and competing capital flows toward high-performing semiconductor stocks—continue to keep major tokens below their earlier peak levels for the year.
