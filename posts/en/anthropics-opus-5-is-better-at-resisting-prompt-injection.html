Anthropic's Opus 5 Sets New Security Standard with Superior Prompt Injection Resistance

www.schneier.com · 2026-07-31T17:23:16Z

Anthropic's Claude Opus 5 model demonstrated industry-leading resistance to prompt injection attacks on the IPI benchmark, drastically outperforming both internal predecessors and rival models.

Artificial intelligence developer Anthropic has released benchmark results demonstrating that its new Claude Opus 5 model offers significantly enhanced defense against prompt injection attacks. According to evaluations on the Indirect Prompt Injection (IPI) benchmark, Opus 5 made marked improvements over its predecessor, Opus 4.8. Specifically, the probability of a successful cyber attack within 15 attempts dropped from 5.5 percent to just 2.0 percent. Furthermore, on a single attack attempt, the model's vulnerability fell from 0.5 percent down to 0.2 percent, establishing a new baseline for frontier model safety.

Within Anthropic’s own lineup, Opus 5 emerged as the most resilient system evaluated, outperforming Sonnet 5, which registered a 5.9 percent vulnerability rate at 15 attempts, and Mythos 5 at 2.6 percent. The gap between Anthropic and rival developers was even more pronounced. The most robust non-Claude model in the evaluation, Muse Spark, yielded a 16.5 percent success rate over 15 attempts. This means attackers were more than eight times as likely to breach Muse Spark compared to Claude Opus 5, underscoring Anthropic's technological lead in structural model defenses.

Competitor architectures showed considerably higher susceptibility to manipulation under the IPI benchmark testing. OpenAI's leading GPT 5.6 variant, Sol, posted a 20.0 percent breach rate at 15 attempts, performing similarly to its predecessor GPT 5.5 at 20.8 percent. Sol proved ten times more vulnerable than Opus 5 overall. Other variants in the GPT 5.6 suite, Terra and Luna, suffered even worse rates at 30.4 percent and 43.9 percent. Strikingly, a single attack attempt against GPT 5.6 Sol succeeded 3.1 percent of the time, higher than the vulnerability rate of Opus 5 after fifteen full attempts.
