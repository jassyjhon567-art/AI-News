Spending Just 5 to 10 Minutes a Day Can Boost Your Brain Health at Any Age, Study Reveals

www.fastcompany.com · 2026-08-02T09:00:00

A study shows that practicing targeted brain exercises for five to ten minutes daily significantly improves cognitive health and overall brain performance at any age.

A three-year study conducted by researchers at the University of Texas at Dallas Center for BrainHealth, published in the journal Scientific Reports, reveals that dedicating just five to ten minutes a day to targeted brain exercises can significantly enhance cognitive function. By tracking nearly 4,000 participants, the study demonstrates that consistent daily practice yields measurable improvements in mental health across all age groups. Furthermore, researchers discovered no apparent ceiling to these benefits, indicating that long-term commitment to this simple routine leads to cumulative and continuous cognitive gains over time.

Unlike casual pastimes like crossword puzzles, the exercises evaluated in the ongoing Brain Health Project are specifically designed to build cognitive resilience. Participants engage in structured activities, such as practicing how to filter out unnecessary information from daily readings to reduce cognitive overload. The program also offers educational components and coaching, while tracking progress every six months through the Brain Health Index. This comprehensive assessment evaluates thinking skills, emotional balance, and social connectedness, effectively challenging the common narrative that mental decline is an inevitable part of aging.

To achieve similar benefits, individuals can adopt various evidence-based practices into their daily routines. Activities such as learning a foreign language, playing a musical instrument, or dancing have all been shown to stimulate neural pathways and strengthen memory. Additionally, incorporating expert advice on lifestyle choices and neurohealth can further protect brain vitality. Spending just a few minutes each day prioritizing cognitive health is one of the most powerful self-improvement strategies available, serving as a vital investment for busy professionals and individuals of all ages.
