JMGO Launches High-Brightness 4K 120Hz Projector Aimed at Gamers and Sports Fans

www.theverge.com · 2026-08-31T18:00:00-04:00

JMGO has launched its 4K 120Hz Iris Ultra Max projector globally, featuring an innovative dual-iris system designed to deliver high brightness and superior contrast for gaming and sports.

JMGO has officially made its latest flagship home theater device, the Iris Ultra Max all-in-one projector, available to international markets outside China. Designed with high-performance home entertainment in mind, this Google TV-powered projector boasts crisp 4K resolution alongside a fluid 120Hz refresh rate, making it an exceptionally appealing display solution for gaming enthusiasts and live sports fans seeking responsive big-screen visuals.

The device derives its name from an innovative dual-iris optical system engineered to tackle one of the most persistent weaknesses in standard projection hardware: contrast performance. By incorporating two separate physical apertures along the light path, the projector dynamically manages illumination to achieve an impressive claimed native contrast ratio of 10,000:1. This dual-aperture mechanism significantly enhances black levels and image depth without sacrificing the peak brightness needed for well-lit environments.

Beyond its optical advancements, the Iris Ultra Max offers a fully integrated entertainment hub through built-in Google TV. This integration grants users seamless access to streaming platforms, downloadable applications, and smart voice navigation straight out of the box. With its combination of rapid refresh rates, high brightness, and superior contrast control, JMGO positions this all-in-one unit as a versatile centerpiece for modern home entertainment setups.
