Apple's Fall Event: What Lifehacker Readers Expect to See

lifehacker.com · Wed, 29 Jul 2026 21:30:01 +0000

Results from Lifehacker's recent survey reveal that readers expect Apple to announce new iPhone models, updated Apple Watches, and new AirPods during its upcoming fall launch event.

As the autumn season approaches, tech enthusiasts and devoted consumers around the globe are once again turning their focus toward Apple's highly anticipated annual product launch event. To gauge public sentiment and expectations, Lifehacker recently conducted the second round of its popular interactive feature, "The Big Guessing Game," inviting readers to submit their detailed predictions regarding upcoming hardware releases and software updates. The newly compiled results offer a fascinating snapshot of what the broader tech community anticipates from the Cupertino giant's upcoming showcase.

Unsurprisingly, the vast majority of survey participants expressed strong confidence that a new generation of flagship iPhones will headline the presentation. Readers broadly expect these flagship devices to feature faster internal processors, major camera system enhancements, expanded storage options, and potential refinements to their physical design. Alongside smartphones, many respondents predicted updates to the Apple Watch family, highlighting advanced health tracking sensors and extended battery life as key demands. Furthermore, predictions heavily leaned toward new AirPods models and official release schedules for recently previewed operating systems.

While most projections focused on Apple's primary product lines, a notable segment of the audience expressed hope for surprising announcements, such as refreshed Mac computers or expanded smart home devices. Nevertheless, the general consensus remains firmly grounded in evolutionary upgrades to established product lines rather than the introduction of entirely new product categories. As the event date draws nearer, these community insights highlight the enduring excitement and high expectations surrounding Apple's ecosystem, leaving fans eager to see how many predictions ultimately come true.
