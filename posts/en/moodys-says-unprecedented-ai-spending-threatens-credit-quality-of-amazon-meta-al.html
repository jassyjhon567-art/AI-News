Moody’s Warns Unprecedented AI Spending Threatens Credit Quality of Tech Giants

www.cnbc.com · Fri, 24 Jul 2026 22:43:56 GMT

Moody's warns that massive investments in artificial intelligence infrastructure are forcing major tech giants to rely on debt, risking their credit ratings.

Credit rating agency Moody's has issued a warning regarding the massive financial commitments required by the ongoing artificial intelligence boom. According to a recent report, unprecedented levels of capital expenditure on AI infrastructure are beginning to pressure the credit quality of major technology leaders, including Amazon, Meta, and Alphabet. Despite holding vast reserves of cash, these tech giants are facing extraordinary costs associated with developing advanced language models, acquiring specialized hardware, and constructing data centers.

Moody's highlighted that the sheer scale of investment is compelling even the world's wealthiest corporations to look beyond internal cash flow. To sustain their aggressive AI development strategies, companies are increasingly turning to external financing mechanisms. This includes issuing corporate debt, engaging in stock sales, and utilizing off-balance-sheet financing arrangements. The rating agency noted that such financial maneuvers could lead to higher leverage ratios and increased financial vulnerability over the coming years if revenue generation fails to keep pace.

While artificial intelligence promises long-term growth and market dominance, the immediate financial burden presents short-term risks to corporate creditworthiness. Financial analysts emphasize that although tech giants currently maintain strong balance sheets, the transition toward sustained debt-funded expenditure marks a significant shift in corporate strategy. As competition in the AI landscape intensifies, credit rating agencies will be closely monitoring whether these heavy capital investments yield sufficient returns to justify the growing debt load and maintain current credit standings.
