NVIDIA Releases NemotronLabs VoiceChat 11B: An Open Full-Duplex Speech Model with Ultra-Low Latency

www.marktechpost.com · Sun, 09 Aug 2026 23:58:34 +0000

NVIDIA has released NemotronLabs VoiceChat 11B, an open full-duplex speech model featuring ~450ms turn-taking latency and live tool-calling capabilities for real-time conversational AI.

NVIDIA has officially unveiled NemotronLabs VoiceChat 11B, an open-source, full-duplex speech-to-speech artificial intelligence model engineered to enable seamless, real-time voice interactions. Unlike traditional speech processing systems that operate in rigid, sequential steps—requiring users to wait while audio is transcribed and processed—this advanced architecture allows simultaneous listening and speaking capabilities. The release marks a major technological milestone in human-computer interaction, offering a publicly accessible framework for global developers and researchers who are eager to build highly responsive, continuous conversational applications.

A primary highlight of the VoiceChat 11B architecture is its ultra-low turn-taking latency of approximately 448 milliseconds, which closely mimics natural human speech dynamics and virtually eliminates awkward conversational delays. Furthermore, the model comes integrated with native live tool calling capabilities, empowering the AI to query external databases, execute code, fetch real-time information from APIs, or trigger automated digital workflows mid-conversation. Powered by an 11-billion-parameter foundation, the system effectively balances complex contextual comprehension with rapid, low-latency audio execution.

By releasing NemotronLabs VoiceChat 11B under an open model license, NVIDIA aims to democratize access to state-of-the-art conversational voice technology across the global developer community. This strategy enables organizations and independent creators to build tailored, domain-specific voice solutions for diverse applications, including automated customer service representatives, hands-free industrial assistants, and immersive virtual entities in interactive gaming. Ultimately, this milestone sets a new technical benchmark for full-duplex speech models, accelerating the deployment of intelligent, action-capable voice interfaces.
