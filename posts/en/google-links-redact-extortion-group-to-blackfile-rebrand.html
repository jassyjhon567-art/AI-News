Google Links 'Redact' Cyber Extortion Group to 'BlackFile' Rebrand After Affiliate Dispute

www.infosecurity-magazine.com · Fri, 07 Aug 2026 09:40:00 GMT

Google Threat Intelligence has linked the newly emerged Redact cyber extortion group to BlackFile following an affiliate dispute, warning that the group continues to conduct dangerous vishing campaigns.

Cybersecurity researchers at Google Threat Intelligence have formally linked the notorious cyber extortion group previously known as BlackFile to a newly emerged threat actor operating under the brand name Redact. The sudden operational shift appears to have been triggered by a severe internal dispute, during which a disgruntled affiliate allegedly hijacked the group's infrastructure and leaked confidential operational data online. Despite this significant setback and exposure, the threat group rapidly reorganized its assets to launch its rebranded extortion venture.

According to security analysts, Redact has maintained the core tactics that made its predecessor a major corporate threat, particularly its heavy reliance on sophisticated voice phishing, or vishing, campaigns. Cybercriminals associated with the group frequently pose as corporate IT helpdesk personnel or trusted third-party service providers to deceive employees into revealing sensitive login credentials or granting unauthorized remote access. Once inside a targeted victim's enterprise network, the group exfiltrates valuable corporate data before issuing steep ransom demands under threat of public leak.

The emergence of Redact highlights the chaotic yet remarkably resilient structure of contemporary cybercrime networks, where internal betrayals often lead to quick rebrandings rather than permanent shutdowns. Security experts emphasize that despite the change in operational identity, the threat posed to enterprise environments remains exceptionally high. Organizations are strongly urged to strengthen their digital defenses by implementing strict identity verification protocols, restricting administrative remote access tools, and conducting continuous security awareness training to mitigate social engineering attacks.
