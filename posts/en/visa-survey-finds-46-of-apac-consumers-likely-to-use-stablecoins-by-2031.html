Visa Survey: Nearly Half of APAC Consumers May Adopt Stablecoins by 2031

www.analyticsinsight.net · Sat, 10 Oct 2026 16:30:00 +0000

A recent Visa survey reveals that 46% of consumers in the Asia-Pacific region anticipate using stablecoins for everyday transactions by 2031.

A comprehensive survey conducted by global payments leader Visa has revealed that nearly half of consumers across the Asia-Pacific (APAC) region are open to using stablecoins within the next seven years. According to the findings, approximately 46% of surveyed individuals expect to incorporate fiat-pegged digital assets into their everyday financial transactions by 2031. This forecast underscores a notable evolution in consumer sentiment toward alternative payment methods across the region's rapidly modernizing economies.

The growing interest in stablecoins is primarily driven by the search for faster, more cost-efficient solutions for cross-border remittances and international commerce. As mobile connectivity and digital payment infrastructure continue to expand throughout APAC, consumers and merchants alike are seeking instruments that blend the speed and round-the-clock availability of blockchain technology with the relative stability of traditional currencies. By mitigating price volatility, these digital assets present a practical alternative to standard cryptocurrencies for retail usage.

These findings signal substantial implications for traditional financial institutions, fintech platforms, and regulatory authorities. In response to evolving consumer preferences, major payment networks are actively exploring pathways to integrate stablecoins into existing settlement rails. However, long-term mainstream adoption will ultimately hinge on robust regulatory clarity, stringent consumer protection standards, and seamless technical interoperability with established banking systems across diverse regional markets.
