Businesses Turn to Prediction Markets as Bespoke Insurance, Highlighted by LA Restaurant's $60,000 Sports Hedge

fortune.com · Mon, 19 Oct 2026 20:41:17 +0000

A Los Angeles restaurant group placed a $60,000 prediction market trade on Kalshi, demonstrating how businesses are increasingly using event contracts as customized insurance against revenue losses.

Prediction markets are increasingly expanding beyond casual forecasting and political wagering, evolving into novel financial hedging tools for commercial enterprises. In a prominent recent example, a Los Angeles-based hospitality group utilized the federally regulated prediction exchange Kalshi to hedge against potential revenue losses tied to local sports outcomes. According to details shared by the platform, the unnamed business placed a $60,000 position specifically to protect against the possibility of the Los Angeles Dodgers failing to advance to the Major League Baseball World Series.

For sports bars, dining establishments, and broader hospitality venues, major postseason runs generate immense foot traffic, private bookings, and food and beverage sales. A premature playoff exit often leads to immediate and substantial shortfalls compared to projected seasonal earnings. Traditional business interruption insurance policies generally do not cover such nuanced, event-driven financial exposure. By taking a contrarian position on a prediction platform, the restaurant group effectively created custom insurance that would pay out if the team was eliminated, thereby offsetting missed revenue opportunities.

This move exemplifies an emerging trend where commercial entities treat event-contract platforms as accessible instruments for bespoke risk mitigation. Financial and industry observers note that as prediction markets continue to expand in liquidity and regulatory clarity, corporate hedging against localized economic disruptions, cultural events, or unexpected regulatory decisions could become far more prevalent. While these strategies require upfront capital and an understanding of market mechanics, they provide flexible mechanisms for agile businesses managing external uncertainties.
